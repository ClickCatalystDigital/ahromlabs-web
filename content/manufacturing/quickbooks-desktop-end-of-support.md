## What stopped working on May 31, 2026?

Desktop 2023 (Pro Plus, Premier Plus including the Manufacturing & Wholesale edition, Mac Plus and Enterprise 23.0)
was discontinued after May 31, 2026. From
Intuit's page, a company still on it has lost:

| Lost | What that means for a shop |
|---|---|
| Live technical support | No help when a file breaks |
| Critical security updates | The company file and the PC are exposed |
| Payroll tax calculation and forms (Basic, Enhanced, Assisted) | Paychecks may be wrong; Assisted no longer files for you |
| Online bank feeds and online banking | Bank transactions typed or imported by hand |
| Payment processing and eInvoice pay links | Card and check payments handled outside QuickBooks |
| Emailing forms and reports | Invoices and statements sent another way |

The books themselves are still there and still open. The risk grows with each month.

## What are the options?

| Option | What changes | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Upgrade within Desktop (Plus 2024 or Enterprise 24.0) | Little; Plus and Enterprise subscribers get 2024 at no extra cost | You want time to decide |
| QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise | Same interface, up to 40 users, industry editions | Desktop users who need more capacity |
| QuickBooks Online Advanced | Cloud; Intuit offers migration help and a first-year discount | Firms ready to move to the cloud |
| Intuit Enterprise Suite | Intuit's ERP for multi-entity firms; where Intuit's manufacturing features are | Larger, multi-company firms |
| Keep QuickBooks for the books, add a shop system | Accounting stays in QuickBooks; jobs, stock and the floor move to a system built for them | Shops whose problem is the work, not the ledger |

Before choosing, read [QuickBooks Online vs Desktop for manufacturers](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-vs-desktop):
QuickBooks Online doesn't do everything Desktop Enterprise does for inventory and assemblies.

## A move off Desktop is a migration either way

Whatever you pick, plan it like one:

1. Decide what moves: lists (customers, vendors, items), open invoices and bills, and how much history.
2. Freeze item and account changes for the move.
3. Run both for one month-end, and compare the trial balance and stock value.
4. Keep the old file read-only, on a backed-up PC, for the records you must keep.

If the move is also the moment to stop running jobs in spreadsheets, see
[when a business has outgrown QuickBooks](/manufacturing/quickbooks-vs-erp) and
[connecting QuickBooks to a shop system](/manufacturing/quickbooks-integration).
