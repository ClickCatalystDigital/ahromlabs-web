## What happened when QuickBooks Desktop 2023 support ended?

Intuit discontinued Desktop 2023 (Pro Plus, Premier Plus including the Manufacturing & Wholesale edition, Mac Plus
and Enterprise 23.0) after May 31, 2026, so a company still on it has lost these services, per Intuit's
[discontinuation policy](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/feature-preferences/quickbooks-desktop-service-discontinuation-policy/L17cXxlie_US_en_US):

| Lost | What that means for a shop |
|---|---|
| Live technical support | No help when a file breaks |
| Critical security updates | The company file and the PC are exposed |
| Payroll tax calculation and forms (Basic, Enhanced, Assisted) | Paychecks may be wrong; Assisted no longer files for you |
| Online bank feeds and online banking | Bank transactions typed or imported by hand |
| Payment processing and eInvoice pay links | Card and check payments handled outside QuickBooks |
| Emailing forms and reports | Invoices and statements sent another way |

The books themselves are still there and still open. The risk grows with each month.

## Can you keep using an old Desktop version offline?

It opens, but without security updates or any connected service, and only on a machine where it is already
registered. Owners on r/QuickBooks still run versions as old as 2013 that way, and the trouble starts when the PC dies:
reinstalling an unsupported copy depends on finding its old registration files. Keep the old file read-only for
history, not as the live books.

## When does QuickBooks Desktop 2024 lose support?

Intuit hasn't said. Its discontinuation policy page still covers only the 2023 versions (updated August 5, 2026), and
third-party sites give different 2027 dates. Plus and Enterprise subscribers already got version 2024 at no extra
cost; watch Intuit's policy page, not a blog, for the 2024 date.

## What are a manufacturer's options?

Five, with Intuit's list prices from November 2026:

| Option | What changes | Price | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stay on Desktop Plus 2024 or Enterprise 24.0 | Little | Included in an active subscription | You want time to decide |
| QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise | Same interface, up to 40 users, industry editions | From $2,210 a year (Gold) | Desktop users who need more capacity |
| QuickBooks Online Advanced | Cloud; migration help | $340 a month, 50% off the first year when you move from Desktop | Firms ready to move to the cloud |
| Intuit Enterprise Suite | Intuit's multi-entity ERP; where its manufacturing orders are | By quote | Larger, multi-company firms |
| Keep QuickBooks for the books, add a shop system | Accounting stays in QuickBooks; jobs, stock and the floor move to a system built for them | The shop system's cost | Shops whose problem is the work, not the ledger |

Before choosing, read [QuickBooks Online vs Desktop for manufacturers](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-vs-desktop):
QuickBooks Online doesn't do everything Desktop Enterprise does for inventory and assemblies.

## How much does moving to QuickBooks Online Advanced cost?

$340 a month at list price, with half off the first year for a business coming from Desktop, and Intuit says an
implementation consultant is included at no cost for simple migrations. For a made-up 14-person shop that's $4,080 a
year at list, $2,040 in the first year: a saving of $2,040. Advanced covers 25 users.

## Should you move all your Desktop history to QuickBooks Online?

Usually not. Move the lists (customers, vendors, items), open invoices and bills, and opening balances, and keep the
old file read-only for history. The top reply on a recent [r/QuickBooks thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/QuickBooks/comments/1xbs9kl/finally_understand_why_everyone_hates_quickbooks/)
about a painful migration said the same: start fresh with the current year rather than carry years of transactions.

## How do you plan the move?

Like any migration, whichever system you pick:

1. Decide what moves: lists, open invoices and bills, balances, and how much history.
2. Freeze item and account changes for the move.
3. Run both for one month-end, and compare the trial balance and stock value. A $1,250 stock difference found then is
   one recount; found next March, it's a year-end adjustment.
4. Keep the old file read-only, on a backed-up PC, for the records you must keep.

If the move is also the moment to stop running jobs in spreadsheets, see
[when a business has outgrown QuickBooks](/manufacturing/quickbooks-vs-erp) and
[connecting QuickBooks to a shop system](/manufacturing/quickbooks-integration).
