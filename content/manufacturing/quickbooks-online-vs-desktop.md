## Is QuickBooks Online or Desktop better for a manufacturer?

For assemblies, bins and lots, Desktop Enterprise still does more; Online wins when people need the books from
anywhere and nobody wants to look after a server. From Intuit's own pages, read on 5–6 November 2026:

| | QuickBooks Online Plus / Advanced | QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Where it runs | Browser, from anywhere | Installed on your PCs or hosted |
| Pitched for | Advanced: companies "needing industry workflows" and working from anywhere | Complex inventory, "like manufacturers and wholesalers" |
| Inventory | Quantity, FIFO or average cost, reorder points | Plus location tracking down to the bin or pallet, lots and serials with Advanced Inventory (Platinum and Diamond) |
| Assemblies and BOMs | Not in Online; Intuit lists BOM management for Advanced as coming soon | Assemblies and builds, with subassemblies |
| Manufacturing orders | In Intuit Enterprise Suite, not Online | Desktop's own assembly builds |

Details are in [QuickBooks Online inventory](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-inventory) and
[does QuickBooks work for manufacturing](/manufacturing/quickbooks-for-manufacturing).

## How much do QuickBooks Online and Desktop Enterprise cost?

QuickBooks Online lists at $38 (Simple Start), $85 (Essentials), $140 (Plus) and $340 (Advanced) a month on
Intuit's [pricing page](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/), with 50% off the first three months, and a free plan
with no inventory. Desktop Enterprise is sold by yearly subscription "starting at" $2,210 (Gold), $2,717 (Platinum)
or $5,363 (Diamond) on Intuit's [Enterprise pricing page](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/desktop/enterprise/pricing/)
(5 November 2026), with custom pricing by number of users, and hosting and payroll extra.

A worked example for a made-up 12-person shop at list prices: Online Advanced covers 25 users at $340 × 12 = $4,080
a year, and a business migrating from Desktop gets 50% off the first year, so three years cost $2,040 + $4,080 +
$4,080 = $10,200. Desktop Enterprise Platinum starts at $2,717 a year, from $8,151 over three years for the smallest
user band, before more users, hosting and payroll. Get a quote for your user count before comparing.

## How many users does each allow?

QuickBooks Online allows 1 user on Simple Start, 3 on Essentials, 5 on Plus and 25 on Advanced. Desktop Enterprise
allows up to 30 users on Gold and Platinum and up to 40 on Diamond.

## Can QuickBooks Online build assemblies from a bill of materials?

Not today. Intuit's [Desktop and Online page](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/desktop/) lists "BOM management" for
Online Advanced as coming soon, and its manufacturing orders, with single-level bills of materials only, live in
Intuit Enterprise Suite. If you build assemblies from components in Desktop today, Online Plus or Advanced won't
replace that yet.

## Which version tracks inventory by location, bin or lot?

Desktop Enterprise with Advanced Inventory, which Intuit includes in the Platinum and Diamond plans only: three
levels of location (such as row, shelf and bin), serial or lot numbers and expiry dates. QuickBooks Online doesn't
track stock by location.

## When is QuickBooks Online the better choice?

- People need the books from more than one place, and nobody wants to look after a server.
- You resell finished goods and don't build assemblies.
- You're coming off an unsupported Desktop version anyway (see
  [Desktop end of support](/manufacturing/quickbooks-desktop-end-of-support)).

## When is Desktop Enterprise still the better choice?

- You build assemblies from components today and need it to keep working.
- You track stock by location or bin in QuickBooks itself.
- You have large lists (up to a million names and items) or many users at once.

## Do you need QuickBooks to do inventory at all?

Many shops don't: they keep QuickBooks Online for the books and run stock, jobs and the floor in a system built for
that, which sends QuickBooks the accounting result. Then the Online vs Desktop choice is only about accounting. See
[connecting QuickBooks to a shop system](/manufacturing/quickbooks-integration) and our
[inventory and reservations](/services/inventory-and-reservations) system.
