## Which systems sit beside QuickBooks, and which replace it?

Fishbowl and Katana sit beside QuickBooks or Xero; Acumatica, NetSuite and Odoo (with its accounting app) replace it. So
the first question isn't which brand; it's whether your accounting stays where it is.

| | Beside your accounting | Replaces your accounting |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Inventory and production, posting to QuickBooks or Xero | One system for operations and the books |
| Examples | Fishbowl, Katana | Acumatica, NetSuite, Odoo (with its accounting app) |
| Your accountant | Keeps QuickBooks | Moves to the new ledger |
| Effort to start | Lower | Higher: a migration and new close process |

## What does each vendor say it does?

Inventory and production in every case, with different depth and accounting. From each vendor's own pages, read on 3 November 2026. Vendors change plans and features often: confirm in a demo.

| System | In its own words (summarised) | Accounting |
|---|---|---|
| **Fishbowl** | Inventory tracking, warehouse operations and manufacturing; integrates with QuickBooks Online | Beside QuickBooks or Xero |
| **Katana** | Lot, serial and expiry tracking from supplier to customer, across inventory and production; barcode-enabled warehouse tasks | Beside QuickBooks and others |
| **Odoo** | Serial numbers, lots, reservation strategies and cycle counting; FIFO, average, LIFO or standard valuation | Its own, as a separate app |
| **Acumatica** | Distribution and financial application: lot or serial tracking, matrix items, kits, replenishment and forecasting | Its own |
| **NetSuite** | Oracle's cloud ERP. Its site refused automated reading when we checked, so we don't summarise its features here: ask for them in writing | Its own |

Pricing isn't compared here: most of these quote per user and per module, and several don't publish prices.

## What does Intuit itself offer before you leave QuickBooks?

Three steps up, all from Intuit's own pages: QuickBooks Online Advanced ($340 a month list, November 2026), with BOM
management listed as coming soon; QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise, from $2,210 a year, with assemblies and, on its
Platinum and Diamond plans, Advanced Inventory (bins, lots, serials); and Intuit Enterprise Suite, Intuit's
multi-entity ERP, where its manufacturing orders live. Details in
[QuickBooks Online vs Desktop](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-vs-desktop).

## How much does a small business ERP cost?

Most of these quote per user and per module, and several don't publish prices, so compare five-year cost on the same
lines rather than list prices. An illustrative example (made-up prices; replace them with your quotes):

| Five years | ERP that replaces QuickBooks | Inventory app beside QuickBooks |
|---|---:|---:|
| Subscription | 15 users × $120 a month = $108,000 | 6 users × $90 a month = $32,400 |
| Implementation and data migration | $30,000 | $8,000 |
| QuickBooks Online Advanced you keep paying | $0 | $340 × 60 = $20,400 |
| **Total** | **$138,000** | **$60,800** |

The cheaper column isn't automatically the answer: add the hours your team spends on workarounds either way, and the
cost of leaving (see the export test in [the ERP requirements checklist](/manufacturing/erp-requirements-checklist)).

## When does a custom system fit better?

When the way you work is the reason customers buy from you. Off-the-shelf systems are built for the common process;
a system built for you is worth it for an unusual quote, a reservation rule, a trace your customers demand, or several
companies sharing stock. See [custom vs off-the-shelf ERP](/manufacturing/custom-vs-off-the-shelf-erp) for the
trade-offs, and [what a job shop ERP must do](/manufacturing/job-shop-erp) for a shop's checklist.

## How do you choose an ERP in two weeks?

With three of your real orders and a demo of each, on your items:

1. Write down three of your real orders end to end: quote, purchase, receipt, production or pick, ship, invoice.
2. Ask each vendor to run them in a demo, on your items, not theirs.
3. Check the inventory basics from [inventory control for distributors](/manufacturing/distributor-inventory-control):
   cycle counts, lots, valuation, scanning.
4. Ask what the [QuickBooks](/manufacturing/quickbooks-for-manufacturing) link posts, and what happens when it fails.
5. Get data export and the contract's ownership terms in writing before you sign.
