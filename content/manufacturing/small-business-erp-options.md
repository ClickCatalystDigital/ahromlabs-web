## Two kinds of system

The first question isn't which brand; it's whether your accounting stays where it is.

| | Beside your accounting | Replaces your accounting |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Inventory and production, posting to QuickBooks or Xero | One system for operations and the books |
| Examples | Fishbowl, Katana | Acumatica, NetSuite, Odoo (with its accounting app) |
| Your accountant | Keeps QuickBooks | Moves to the new ledger |
| Effort to start | Lower | Higher: a migration and new close process |

## What each says it does

From each vendor's own pages, read on 3 November 2026. Vendors change plans and features often: confirm in a demo.

| System | In its own words (summarised) | Accounting |
|---|---|---|
| **Fishbowl** | Inventory tracking, warehouse operations and manufacturing; integrates with QuickBooks Online | Beside QuickBooks or Xero |
| **Katana** | Lot, serial and expiry tracking from supplier to customer, across inventory and production; barcode-enabled warehouse tasks | Beside QuickBooks and others |
| **Odoo** | Serial numbers, lots, reservation strategies and cycle counting; FIFO, average, LIFO or standard valuation | Its own, as a separate app |
| **Acumatica** | Distribution and financial application: lot or serial tracking, matrix items, kits, replenishment and forecasting | Its own |
| **NetSuite** | Oracle's cloud ERP. Its site refused automated reading when we checked, so we don't summarise its features here: ask for them in writing | Its own |

Pricing isn't compared here: most of these quote per user and per module, and several don't publish prices.

## When a custom system fits

Off-the-shelf systems are built for the common process. A system built for you is worth it when the way you work is
the reason customers buy from you: an unusual quote, a reservation rule, a trace your customers demand, or several
companies sharing stock. See [custom vs off-the-shelf ERP](/manufacturing/custom-vs-off-the-shelf-erp) for the
trade-offs, and [what a job shop ERP must do](/manufacturing/job-shop-erp) for a shop's checklist.

## How to choose in two weeks

1. Write down three of your real orders end to end: quote, purchase, receipt, production or pick, ship, invoice.
2. Ask each vendor to run them in a demo, on your items, not theirs.
3. Check the inventory basics from [inventory control for distributors](/manufacturing/distributor-inventory-control):
   cycle counts, lots, valuation, scanning.
4. Ask what the [QuickBooks](/manufacturing/quickbooks-for-manufacturing) link posts, and what happens when it fails.
5. Get data export and the contract's ownership terms in writing before you sign.
