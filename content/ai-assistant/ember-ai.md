## What is Ember AI?

Ember AI is the assistant built into SB Ops, the operations system we built for a boiler manufacturer and run as two
public demos (India and US) with a made-up company. It answers in the words people use on the shop floor, from the
system's own records, and it keeps each department to its own data: stores sees stock, procurement sees purchase
orders, accounts sees payments, and nobody sees what their login can't see on screen.

## What does Ember AI answer?

| Department | Example questions |
|---|---|
| Owner and managers | "Is a project delayed?" "What approvals are waiting for me?" |
| Procurement | "Which purchase orders haven't been received?" |
| Stores | "Which items are below their minimum?" "How much of a material do we have in stock?" |
| Accounts | "Which customers owe us the most?" "What is an order worth and how much has been paid?" |
| Quality and engineering | "How do I assign test certificates to units?" "How do I release the BOM?" |
| Sales | "How do I convert a quotation into a sale order?" |

How-to questions are answered everywhere. Live-data questions answer on the demo with the sample company; at the
client they are switched off until the client approves them. The full list, with the status of each, is in
[AI assistant for manufacturers](/ai-assistant/manufacturers) and the [question library](/ai-assistant/questions).

## What can Ember AI change?

It prepares changes as cards: add an enquiry ("a company wants a 2 TPH boiler"), log a meeting and set the follow-up
("they want the revised offer by Monday"), add tasks and more. The owner can switch on Auto for chosen roles, with a
30-minute Undo; deletes, payments and approvals always ask. See [actions and approvals](/ai-assistant/actions-and-approvals).

## What is the steel purchase what-if?

A calculator for the question every boiler maker argues about: buy steel now, or later? You enter the prices, rates
and dates; it shows the cost of each choice with the assumptions listed. Ember AI points to it rather than guessing.
It is live on the demo for the owner, sales and marketing roles. See
[predictions and recommendations](/ai-assistant/predictions-and-recommendations).

## Can I use Ember AI from ChatGPT or Claude?

Yes, on a client's system where it is switched on: managers and admins add SB Ops as a connector and ask Ember AI from
the AI app, by text or, where the app supports it, by voice. Changes still need their yes. The connector is off in the
public demos. See [MCP for ERP](/ai-assistant/mcp-for-erp).

## What is planned next?

Written into Ember AI as cards marked "planned": which open orders are exposed if steel prices rise before purchase,
what the cash gap on an order will cost, which delayed milestones will hold up dispatch, and what to deal with first
this week. See [market signals](/ai-assistant/market-signals) for the outside data these will use.
