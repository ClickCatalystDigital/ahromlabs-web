## How is a boiler certificate renewed under IBR 2026?

The owner applies for renewal with the fee under regulation 305, prepares the boiler for inspection, and the
Competent Person inspects it; if it is fit, a fresh certificate for use is issued in [Form XII](/ibr/forms/form-xii).
Boilers for electric power generation and waste heat boilers in continuous process plants follow Appendix H and
Appendix I, and their application carries the details in [Form IX](/ibr/forms/form-ix): feed water quality, safety
valves, protection device tests, stoppages and past repairs.

## How must the boiler be prepared?

Regulation 295 is specific: "At every boiler examination or grant of renewal of a certificate, the boiler shall be
empty and all its parts shall be thoroughly cleaned." Manhole, handhole and sight hole doors, cleaning plugs, header
and mud drum caps, fire bars, bearers, bridges, arches, burners and stoker fittings come off, and "All valves and cocks
comprising the boiler mounting shall be opened up and taken apart". Smoke tubes, the outside of water tubes, smoke
boxes and flues are swept; the boiler is disconnected from any other boiler under steam; no blank flange may sit
between a safety valve chest and the boiler.

Large boilers get some relief: for boilers of 90 tonnes per hour or more on treated feed water, at alternate annual
inspections the Competent Person may relax the preparation, provided at least the three bottom rows, or all tubes in
the first pass of heat, are opened up.

## What happens if the boiler isn't ready?

The inspection doesn't happen. Under regulation 311, if the boiler isn't properly disconnected, cleaned or descaled,
or is too hot, the Competent Person "shall decline to proceed with the inspection" and reports it for orders. A
re-inspection of a defective boiler after repairs pays the fee again (regulation 305(5)).

## What does the Competent Person check?

Every accessible part, inside and out (regulation 311): going inside where the size allows; signs of oil or scale,
which the regulation calls "particularly bad"; corrosion and grooving along seams, which is more serious than the
same corrosion in solid plate; the seating blocks in damp settings; and for forced circulation boilers, that water
flows through every section. Where wear is suspected, insulation or masonry may be removed and plates drilled to
find the real thickness; a wasted shell is recalculated under regulation 312.

## When is a hydraulic test needed at renewal?

At 1.25 times the design pressure when one is required: "Hydraulic tests of boilers at subsequent examination shall
be at 1.25 times the design pressure of the boiler." Water tube, locomotive type and all tubular boilers are tested at
each inspection for the grant or renewal of a certificate unless the Chief Inspector waives it, and a boiler the
Competent Person can't get inside is tested at 1.5 times the design pressure. The Competent Person may order a test at
any inspection. Compare the [ASME hydrostatic test](/asme/hydrostatic-test).

## What does renewal cost?

The fee in regulation 305 by heating surface; for heat recovery steam generators with finned tubes, on the bare tube
area plus a quarter of the fin area. "For renewal of certificate for a period of six months or less on the request of
owner, half of applicable fee shall be payable."

## How should an owner keep track?

By the certificate's expiry, the inspection date booked well before it, the preparation done and checked, and the
inspection notes and hydraulic test entered in the boiler's [Memorandum of Inspection Book](/ibr/forms/form-xi). Keep
the records for the boiler's life, as [regulation 308](/ibr/record-keeping) requires. Our boiler ERP tracks installed
boilers due for service and keeps each boiler's IBR folder; see
[ERP for boiler and pressure vessel shops](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing).
