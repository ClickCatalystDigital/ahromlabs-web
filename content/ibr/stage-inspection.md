## What is stage inspection under IBR 2026?

Stage inspection is the set of points during manufacture at which the Inspecting Authority, through a Competent
Person it nominates, must inspect a boiler before work goes on. IBR 2026 lists them in Appendix A, by type of boiler
and component. The Inspecting Authority may also inspect at any other stage: "In case of any doubt, the Inspecting
Authority or Competent Person may examine at any stage other than the stages stipulated below."

These are IBR's hold points. In ASME terms, they are the Inspector's points on the job's
[inspection and test plan](/asme/inspection-and-test-plan).

## How much notice must the manufacturer give?

At least four working days before each stage. Appendix A says: "The manufacturer shall give at least four working
days' notice to the Inspecting Authority or Competent Person before reaching each stage." Before inspecting, the
Competent Person also checks that the testing equipment and instruments are calibrated, so keep calibration
certificates with the job.

## What are the stages for a shell type boiler?

| Stage | When |
|---|---|
| (a) Material | Materials ready for identification against their test certificates; plate marks laid out to stay visible, or transferred if cut out; weld test plate material identified |
| (b) Forming | Shell and end plates formed, edges prepared for welding, test plates attached |
| (c) Shell welding | Main cylindrical shell welded and checked for circularity |
| (d) NDE | Radiographs and NDE reports examined |
| (e) Openings | Openings prepared and stand pipes, connections and end plates tack welded, and again on completion |
| (f) Heat treatment | Drum or shell welding complete; heat treatment records checked where required |
| (g) Test specimens | Weld test specimens from the test plate, to witness the testing |
| (h) Hydraulic test | "During hydraulic test, followed by external and internal examination and stamping." |

## What are the stages for a water tube boiler?

Welded drums, headers, separators and vessels follow the same pattern with more steps: material identification;
plates formed and set up for welding with test plates; shell welded, checked for circularity, with RT or UT records;
end plates identified, formed and set up; end plate welds complete with their records; compensation plates and
attachments, with at least 10% of each type of branch or tube stub set up for welding; all welding complete with heat
treatment records and test specimens marked off; NDE after stress relieving for alloy steel drums and carbon steel
drums over 0.25% carbon or 100 mm thick; and the hydraulic test with final examination and stamping.

Seamless drums and headers skip the shell seam stages. Tubular and piping components have three: material
identification, welding complete with NDE and stress relieving records, and the hydraulic test.

## What is inspected at the steel maker, foundry, forge and tube maker?

Each makes its own stages, from chemistry through NDE, mechanical tests, final examination and stamping. When the
maker is a well-known steel maker, foundry, forge or tube maker recognised by the Central Boilers Board, it carries out
and certifies the tests itself: for steel makers, "these tests shall be carried out and certified by the Steel Makers
themselves and the records maintained properly." Their certificates come as
[Form V Part A](/ibr/forms/form-v-part-a), [Form IV Part E](/ibr/forms/form-iv-part-e) for castings,
[Form IV Part F](/ibr/forms/form-iv-part-f) for forgings and [Form IV Part B](/ibr/forms/form-iv-part-b) or
[Part C](/ibr/forms/form-iv-part-c) for pipes and tubes. Valves and mountings have two stages: material
identification, then the hydraulic test and stamping, certified on [Form IV Part D](/ibr/forms/form-iv-part-d).

## What breaks at stage inspection?

- A plate's identification mark cut out and not transferred. Appendix A requires it to be "transferred by the
  manufacturer to another part of the component to the satisfaction of the Inspecting Authority or Competent Person".
- A stage reached without notice, so welding goes on before the Competent Person has seen the set-up.
- NDE reports or heat treatment charts not ready when the Competent Person arrives.
- A test gauge without a current calibration certificate.

## How should a boiler maker track the stages?

As a plan per boiler: each Appendix A stage as a step that can't be passed until the Competent Person has signed it,
the notice date recorded, and the records (test certificates, NDE, heat treatment, hydro test) attached to the stage
as they are made. The same records then make up the [registration documents](/ibr/registration-documents) and the
folder kept under [regulation 308](/ibr/record-keeping). Our boiler ERP keeps the job's inspections and NCRs against
the job and prints the IBR folder from them; the US build enforces the hold points in the server. See
[ERP for boiler and pressure vessel shops](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing).
