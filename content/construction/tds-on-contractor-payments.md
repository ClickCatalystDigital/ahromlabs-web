## What changed in April 2026?

The Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026. TDS on payments to contractors,
section 194C under the old Act, is now a row of the table in section 393: serial number 6, "Payments to contractors,
fees for professional and technical services, etc." The rates and thresholds below are read from that table.

## Rates and thresholds

| Who pays | Contractor | Rate | Deduct when |
|---|---|---|---|
| A designated person (companies, firms, government and others the Act lists) | Individual or HUF | 1% | A single payment is over ₹30,000, or the year's total is over ₹1,00,000 |
| A designated person | Anyone else (company, firm, LLP) | 2% | Same thresholds |
| An individual or HUF not otherwise required to deduct (serial 6(ii)) | Any contractor | 2% | Payments for work over ₹50 lakh in the year |

"Work" covers any work under a contract, including supplying labour for it. The Act's definition also brings in
advertising, broadcasting, carriage of goods or passengers other than by rail, catering, and making a product to a
customer's specification with material bought from that customer, but not with material bought from someone else.

Rates in the table can change in a Finance Act. Check the table as amended for the year you pay in.

## How it sits on an RA bill

TDS is deducted from each [running account bill](/construction/ra-bills) at the rate in the table. Whether GST
shown separately on the invoice is part of the amount follows CBDT's guidance; confirm the position under the 2025
Act with your adviser. A main contractor paying its subcontractors deducts the same way. The deduction is deposited and reported in the TDS return, and the
contractor claims credit for it against its own tax.

Government and certain other buyers also deduct TDS under GST, which is separate from income-tax TDS; see
[GST on works contracts](/construction/gst-on-works-contracts).

## What your system should do

- Hold each contractor's status (individual or HUF, or other) and PAN, which sets the rate.
- Track payments to each contractor across the year, so the ₹1,00,000 total is caught on the bill that crosses it.
- Deduct on the base your adviser confirms, and show it as its own line on the
  [measurement-based bill](/construction/measurement-book).
- Produce the challan and return data per contractor, and the certificate they need.
