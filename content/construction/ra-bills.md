## What is an RA bill?

A running account (RA) bill is an interim bill for the work measured so far, paid while the work continues; the
final bill closes the contract. On an item-rate contract the contractor is paid by measured quantity at the agreed
rate for each BOQ item, so the work is billed in stages: the 1st RA bill, the 2nd, and so on. CPWD's general
conditions treat every such payment as an advance against the final payment, so an RA bill doesn't mean the work has
been accepted. Most private contracts in India follow the same pattern, with the rates and deductions their own
contract sets.

## How is an RA bill calculated?

From the [measurement book](/construction/measurement-book), cumulatively: the value of all work measured to date at
the agreement rates, plus approved extra and deviated items, less the gross of all earlier bills. The deductions are
then taken from this bill's value.

| Line | Example (made up) |
|---|---|
| Value of work done to date, item by item at agreement rates | ₹48,00,000 |
| Add: extra and deviated items approved | ₹1,50,000 |
| Gross value to date | ₹49,50,000 |
| Less: gross of previous RA bills | ₹31,00,000 |
| **Gross value of this bill** | **₹18,50,000** |

## Which works are paid by RA bills on CPWD contracts?

Works estimated to cost more than ₹20 lakh. Under Clause 7 of CPWD's General Conditions of Contract 2023, a work
estimated at ₹20 lakh or less is paid only after the whole work is completed and the completion certificate given.
Bigger works are billed monthly on recorded measurements, on the date the Engineer-in-Charge fixes, and a bill is due
only once the work done since the last one reaches the minimum set in the contract's Schedule F.

## What is deducted from an RA bill?

Up to five things, each set by the contract or the law:

- **Security deposit (retention money):** a percentage of each bill held until the defects period ends.
- **Income-tax TDS** at 1% or 2%: see [TDS on payments to contractors](/construction/tds-on-contractor-payments).
- **GST TDS**, when the client is a government department or agency: 1% CGST plus 1% SGST; see
  [GST on works contracts](/construction/gst-on-works-contracts).
- **Recovery of advances:** mobilisation advance with its interest, and secured advance once the material is used.
- **Recoveries for materials the client issued**, water, electricity, and any penalties the contract allows.

## How much security deposit is deducted from an RA bill?

On CPWD works, 2.5% of the gross amount of each running and final bill, until 2.5% of the tendered amount has been
held (GCC Clause 1A). On a ₹2 crore work that is ₹5 lakh in all; a ₹18,50,000 bill gives up ₹46,250. Private
contracts usually call it retention money and set their own rate. CPWD lets the deposit be released against a bank
guarantee from a scheduled bank once at least ₹5 lakh has built up.

## When is the security deposit refunded?

After the defect liability period, or once the final bill is passed, whichever is later. Under GCC Clause 17 that
period is 12 months after the completion certificate, or 6 months for works costing ₹10 lakh or less (except road
works); the contractor must make good defects that appear in it, and the department can pay for them from the
deposit. Clause 1 and the release of security deposit have been amended by CPWD circulars since 2023; check the
contract's own GCC version.

## What is a mobilisation advance, and how is it recovered?

An advance to set up the work (site office, labour camp, testing lab, godown, plant and machinery, shuttering), up to
10% of the tendered amount, when the contract provides for it. Under GCC Clause 10B it's asked for in writing within
six months of the order to start, paid in instalments of no more than 5% each (the next only after the last is shown
to be used), and carries simple interest at 8% a year. Recovery starts once 10% of the work's gross value has been
executed and paid, in proportion to each bill, so that all of it is back by the time 80% has been executed and paid.

On a ₹2 crore work with a ₹20 lakh advance, recovery runs over the ₹1.4 crore of work between 10% and 80%: 14.29% of
each bill's gross value. Interest is charged on the amount still outstanding up to each recovery.

## What is a secured advance?

An advance on materials brought to site for work not yet done: up to 75% of the assessed value of materials that are
non-perishable, non-fragile and non-combustible, properly stored and not yet built in (GCC Clause 10B). When the
material is used in the work, the advance is recovered from the next payment. It isn't paid on high-risk materials
such as ordinary glass, sand, petrol or diesel; perishable or fragile materials qualify only with insurance and the
Engineer-in-Charge's approval. Cement and steel on site assessed at ₹4,00,000 give an advance of ₹3,00,000, recovered
in full from the bill in which they are built in.

## What does a full RA bill look like with every deduction?

RA bill #3 on a made-up ₹2 crore CPWD-style work, contractor a company, client a government department:

| Line | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Gross value of this bill | 18,50,000 |
| Add GST at 18% (the contractor's tax invoice) | 3,33,000 |
| **Invoice value** | **21,83,000** |
| Less security deposit, 2.5% of the gross | 46,250 |
| Less mobilisation advance recovered (14.29% of the gross) | 2,64,286 |
| Less interest on the advance outstanding (₹18,42,857 × 8% × 31 days) | 12,521 |
| Less income-tax TDS at 2% on the value excluding GST | 37,000 |
| Less GST TDS, 1% CGST + 1% SGST | 37,000 |
| **Net payable** | **17,85,943** |

The figures are made up; the days of interest are illustrative. Recovery of the advance started on bill #2, so
₹1,57,143 of it had already come back.

## How soon must an RA bill be paid?

On CPWD works, at least 75% of the net bill as an ad-hoc payment within 10 working days of submission, and the rest
after final checking within 28 working days (GCC Clause 7). If a bill that's in order isn't paid within 45 days of
submission, the department pays simple interest at 5% a year from the end of the time limit. On the bill above, the
ad-hoc payment is at least ₹13,39,457.

For a private client: if you're a registered micro or small enterprise, the MSMED Act caps the agreed credit period
at 45 days from acceptance; see the [MSME payment rule](/systems/msme-payment-rule).

## When must the final bill be submitted and paid?

Within three months of physical completion, or one month of the final completion certificate, whichever is earlier
(GCC Clause 9). Once it's submitted, no further claims can be made: they are treated as waived. The undisputed items,
and disputed ones at the approved quantity and rate, are paid within three months of the bill being received, with
5% a year interest if the department is late on a bill submitted in time. Work completed on 15 January 2027 with the
final certificate on 10 March 2027 needs its final bill by 10 April 2027.

## Can I get paid before the work is measured?

Sometimes. The CPWD manual allows advance payments on a running bill for work measured but not yet checked, or done
but not yet measured, on conditions, adjusted when the measurements are recorded. GCC Clause 7 also lets the
Engineer-in-Charge pay 75% of the assessed value of a building floor's work up to lintel or slab level without
detailed measurement, adjusted in the next bill.

## What should a contractor's system track on RA bills?

- The BOQ with agreement rates, and each RA bill taken from the measurements, never typed totals.
- Every bill cumulative, so this bill is always to date less previous.
- Each deduction by type across bills: security deposit held, advances still to recover with their interest, TDS and
  GST TDS deducted.
- Extra and deviated items kept apart until approved, so they're billed once.
- Due dates for the ad-hoc payment, the balance and the final bill, and the refund date of the security deposit.
