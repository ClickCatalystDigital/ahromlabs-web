title: "Hold point"
answer: >
  A step on an inspection and test plan that work may not pass until a named party (shop QC, the Authorized
  Inspector or the customer's inspector) has inspected it and released it. A witness point needs only notice; a hold
  point stops the job.

Hold points are written on the job's [inspection and test plan](/asme/inspection-and-test-plan) beside witness (W), review (R) and surveillance (S) points; ASME doesn't define the letters, the purchase order or the shop's procedure does. Typical holds are material receipt, fit-up before welding, NDE before a joint is covered, and the hydrostatic test. A hold only works if the next step is actually refused until it is released, and stays refused while a [nonconformance report](/systems/nonconformance-report) is open.
