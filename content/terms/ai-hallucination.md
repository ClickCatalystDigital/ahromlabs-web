title: "AI hallucination"
answer: >
  When an AI model states something that isn't in the data it was given, such as a total it added up wrong, a
  rounded figure or a name it invented, in the same confident tone as a true answer.
domain: [truth]
order: 12
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

In a business system the dangerous kind is a number that is nearly right. The way to rule it out is to keep the model away from the numbers, as described in [how an AI assistant answers from your records](/ai-assistant/how-it-works); the way to catch it is a [grounding check](/systems/grounding-check), which you can run yourself with the [number check](/ai-assistant/number-check).
