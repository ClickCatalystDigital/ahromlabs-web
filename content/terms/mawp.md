title: "MAWP (maximum allowable working pressure)"
answer: >
  The highest gauge pressure a finished boiler or pressure vessel may run at, measured at its top, at a stated
  temperature, worked out from the thicknesses actually used less corrosion. The relief device is set at or
  below it.
domain: [pressure]
order: 1
published: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-01
---

It is at least the design pressure and is stamped on the nameplate. How it relates to design and operating pressure is in [MAWP and design pressure](/asme/mawp); the [shell thickness calculator](/asme/shell-thickness) works it back from a thickness.
