title: "MAWP (maximum allowable working pressure)"
answer: >
  The highest gauge pressure a finished boiler or pressure vessel may run at, measured at its top, at a stated
  temperature, worked out from the thicknesses actually used less corrosion. The relief device is set at or
  below it.
domain: [pressure]
order: 1
published: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because the relief valve, the hydrostatic test and the nameplate all follow from it: the hydro test is at least 1.3 times it for a Section VIII Division 1 vessel. Standard plate is often thicker than needed, so MAWP can be higher than the design pressure; worked from ⅜ in plate, a 48 in shell designed for 150 psi allows about 168 to 175 psi.

How it relates to design and operating pressure is in [MAWP and design pressure](/asme/mawp); the [shell thickness calculator](/asme/shell-thickness) works it back from a thickness, and the [hydrostatic test calculator](/asme/hydrostatic-test) takes it forward.
