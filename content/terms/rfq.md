  A request for a price and lead time on a defined part or quantity. A manufacturer receives RFQs from customers and sends them to suppliers; the two are different workflows.

- **Customer RFQ** (incoming): a drawing, quantity and due date arrive by email or a portal; the shop works out material, operations and times, and sends a quote. Speed matters: same-day quotes win work.
- **Supplier RFQ** (outgoing): the shop asks vendors to price material or outside processing, compares the replies and orders.

An RFQ asks for a price on something already specified; an RFP (request for proposal) asks suppliers to propose how they would meet a need, and is judged on more than price. The quoted operations from a customer RFQ become the job's [routing](/systems/routing) if the order comes in. For supplier quotes collected on a link instead of by email, see [suppliers quote on a link](/notes/suppliers-quote-on-a-link).
