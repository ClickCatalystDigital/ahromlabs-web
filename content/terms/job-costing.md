Job costing suits work made to order, where every job is different; process costing averages cost over a continuous run of identical units. In a machine shop the useful comparison is per operation: quoted against actual setup and run time, because that is what the next quote can correct. A worked example with a calculator is in [job costing in QuickBooks for manufacturers](/manufacturing/quickbooks-job-costing).
