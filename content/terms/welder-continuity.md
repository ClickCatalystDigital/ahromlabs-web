title: "Welder continuity"
answer: >
  The rule (ASME Section IX, QW-322) that a welder's qualification for a process lapses after six months without
  welding with it. Shops keep a log of each welder's last weld per process to show continuity.
domain: [pressure]
order: 7
published: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because a lapse found after welding means the welds were made by an unqualified welder: a nonconformity, and often a cut-out and reweld. The clock runs per process, not per WPS, and a lapsed qualification is renewed with a single test coupon (QW-322.2). An Authorized Inspector checks continuity for every welder whose stamp is on a job.

A free checker, an Excel log and the record to keep are in [welder continuity](/asme/welder-continuity). Keeping each welder's last weld per process from the shop's own records is part of [material traceability](/services/material-traceability).
