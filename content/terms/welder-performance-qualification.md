answer: >
  The record that a welder passed their own qualification test under ASME Section IX, and what it covers:
  process, positions, thicknesses, diameters and filler F-number.

It matters because a welder may only weld within what their WPQ covers: a 6G pipe test covers all positions, a plate test covers pipe only over 24 in outside diameter. It doesn't expire on a date, but lapses after six months without the process, and each organization is responsible for the qualifications its welders work under. ASME's suggested formats are QW-484A (welders) and QW-484B (welding operators).

See [welder qualification](/asme/welder-qualification) and [welder continuity](/asme/welder-continuity). Under IBR in India, boiler welders also need an IBR welder certificate; see [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).
