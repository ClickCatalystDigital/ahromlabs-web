WIP matters twice. On the floor, it shows where each job is and where work is piling up in front of a machine. In the books, it is an asset: the cost of material issued and hours worked on open jobs, which stays on the balance sheet until the job ships. QuickBooks Online has no WIP stage of its own, so material sits in stock until someone adjusts it; see [QuickBooks Online inventory for manufacturers](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-inventory). Tracking it means recording each operation as it finishes, the same data [job costing](/manufacturing/quickbooks-job-costing) uses.
