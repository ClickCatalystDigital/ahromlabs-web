title: "Descriptive analytics"
answer: >
  Analytics that answers what happened: totals, counts and lists from records, such as billing this month, stock by store or dues by customer.
domain: [truth]
order: 24
published: 2026-11-14

It is the first of four levels (descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive) and everything else rests on it: a forecast built on wrong history is wrong. In an ERP assistant it means fixed look-ups with the same maths as the screens.

See [the four levels of analytics](/ai-assistant/analytics-levels).
