title: "E-way bill"
answer: >
  The electronic document generated on the GST e-way bill system before goods move, required under rule 138 of the CGST Rules for a consignment value exceeding ₹50,000.
domain: [operations]
order: 3
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because goods can't move without one above the threshold, and retyping the consignment into the portal at the gate is where errors and delays start. Under rule 138 of the CGST Rules the threshold is a consignment value exceeding ₹50,000; the ERP should produce the e-way bill from the invoice and dispatch record.

See [ERP compliance in India](/erp/compliance) and the [e-way bill requirement](/erp/requirements#dispatch-eway-bill). Ahrom Labs has built a direct NIC client to the official v1.03 spec, live once a client's NIC account is set up.
