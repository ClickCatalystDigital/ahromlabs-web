title: "E-way bill"
answer: >
  The electronic document generated on the GST e-way bill system before goods move, required under rule 138 of the CGST Rules for a consignment value exceeding ₹50,000.
domain: [operations]
order: 3
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

An ERP should produce the e-way bill from the invoice and dispatch record rather than having someone retype the consignment into the portal at the gate. See the [e-way bill requirement](/erp/requirements#dispatch-eway-bill); Ahrom Labs has built a direct NIC client to the official v1.03 spec, live once a client's NIC account is set up.
