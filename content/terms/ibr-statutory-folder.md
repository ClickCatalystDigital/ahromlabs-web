title: "IBR statutory folder"
answer: >
  The set of documents a boiler maker sends with each boiler for registration under the Indian Boiler
  Regulations: a covering letter, the forms, the mountings and fittings list, a stage-wise inspection report and
  copies of the material test certificates, filed in a fixed order.
domain: [operations]
order: 17
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because a boiler can't be registered without it, and assembling it by hand from loose certificates is where days are lost and copies go missing. Under IBR 2026 the forms are Form I Part B or C, Form IV Part A and Form V Part A with its Part B extract, and a boiler manufacturer keeps the records for at least five years (regulation 308(4)).

See [documents for boiler registration](/ibr/registration-documents), [record keeping under IBR 2026](/ibr/record-keeping) and [the IBR statutory folder](/notes/ibr-statutory-folder-from-bom); a boiler maker's system assembles it from a bank of material test certificates linked to each part. The ASME Code's counterpart is the [Manufacturer's Data Report](/asme/data-reports).
