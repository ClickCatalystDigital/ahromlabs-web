answer: >
  Working out what material to buy or make, how much and when, from orders, bills of materials, stock on hand and lead times.

MRP explodes each order through its [bill of materials](/systems#bill-of-materials), subtracts stock and open purchase orders, and offsets by lead time to say "order this, by then". Classic MRP assumes unlimited machine capacity; finite scheduling (see [production scheduling software](/manufacturing/production-scheduling-software)) is the step that checks the plan against real machine hours. ERP grew out of MRP by adding sales, purchasing and accounting on the same database.
