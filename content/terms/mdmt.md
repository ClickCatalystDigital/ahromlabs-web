title: "MDMT (minimum design metal temperature)"
answer: >
  The coldest metal temperature at which a pressure vessel may be at its full pressure. It decides whether the
  steel, welds and heat-affected zones must be impact tested to prove they won't fracture when cold.
domain: [pressure]
order: 2
published: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-01
---

It is stamped on the nameplate with the MAWP. The exemption curves and when testing is needed are in [MDMT and impact testing](/asme/impact-testing).
