title: "WPS (welding procedure specification)"
answer: >
  The written welding instructions a welder follows: process, materials, filler metal, position, preheat, heat
  treatment and electrical settings, each given as a range supported by a qualified procedure test (the PQR).
domain: [pressure]

It matters because every Code weld must be made to a qualified WPS, and the Inspector checks that the seam's thickness, material and heat treatment sit inside its ranges. A WPS is written from one or more PQRs, revised for nonessential changes and requalified for essential ones. ASME's suggested format is QW-482.

How it relates to the PQR and the welder's qualification is in [WPS vs PQR vs WPQ](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq); which changes force a new test is in [essential variables](/asme/essential-variables). A shop's weld map should show the WPS used on each seam; see [ERP for boiler and pressure vessel shops](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing).
