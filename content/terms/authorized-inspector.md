answer: >
  The independent inspector on every ASME-stamped boiler or pressure vessel, employed by an accredited
  inspection agency and holding a National Board commission. They check design, materials, welding and testing,
  and sign the data report.

No Inspector's signature, no stamp. A shop can't hire one directly: it signs an agreement with an inspection agency accredited by ASME under QAI-1, which assigns an inspector holding the National Board's Authorized Inspector (AI) commission, renewed every year. The Inspector picks welds and walks them back through the records, witnesses the pressure test and signs the data report, so a shop's records decide how fast a job closes.

See [the Authorized Inspector and AIA](/asme/authorized-inspector) and [how to get the U stamp](/asme/u-stamp). The records the Inspector checks are what a [boiler and vessel shop's system](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing) should keep.
