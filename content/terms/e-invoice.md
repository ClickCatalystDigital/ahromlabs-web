title: "E-invoice (IRN)"
answer: >
  A B2B invoice, credit note or debit note registered on the GST invoice registration portal (IRP), which returns an invoice reference number (IRN) and a signed QR code.
domain: [operations]
order: 2

Registered persons whose aggregate turnover exceeded ₹5 crore in any financial year from 2017-18 must e-invoice from 1 August 2023 (Notification 10/2023–Central Tax). From 1 April 2025, taxpayers with annual aggregate turnover of ₹10 crore and above cannot report a document to the IRP more than 30 days after its date (GSTN advisory, 5 November 2024).

See the [e-invoice requirements](/erp/requirements#accounts-e-invoice). Ahrom Labs has not yet built an IRP connection.
