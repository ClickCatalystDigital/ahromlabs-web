title: "Human in the loop"
answer: >
  A design in which an AI system prepares an action but a person reviews and approves it before it takes effect.
domain: [truth]
order: 22
published: 2026-11-14
updated: 2026-11-14
---

In a business system it means the assistant shows a preview (which record, which fields, which values) and saves only when the person presses Save. Risky changes such as deletes, payments and approvals keep the person in the loop always; routine adds and edits can be saved at once for chosen roles if an Undo is available.

See [actions and approvals](/ai-assistant/actions-and-approvals).
