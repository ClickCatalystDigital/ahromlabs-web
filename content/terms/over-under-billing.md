title: "Over and under billing"
answer: >
  The gap between what a contractor has billed on a job and what it has earned by percentage of completion: billing ahead of progress is over billing (a liability), behind it is under billing (an asset).
domain: [operations]
order: 25
published: 2026-11-05
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because it is the earliest warning that a job is losing money, and because banks and surety companies read it on the WIP schedule to judge a contractor. Percent complete is cost to date over estimated total cost; earned revenue is that share of the contract value; billings above it are over billing, below it under billing.

How a work-in-progress schedule finds both, with a worked example, is in [the WIP schedule](/construction/wip-schedule); the billing it is measured against is in [pay applications](/construction/pay-applications).
