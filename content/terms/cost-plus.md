title: "Cost plus"
answer: >
  A way of pricing product: the seller buys at cost and charges the client that cost plus an agreed percentage, the markup.
domain: [operations]
order: 26
published: 2026-11-07
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because the percentage is a markup on cost, not a margin on the price: 35% on $40,000 of furniture is a $54,000 price and a 25.9% margin. Interior design firms often price product this way beside an hourly or flat design fee, and the contract has to say what "cost" means (net of the trade discount, before or after freight).

See [how to charge for interior design](/interiors/how-to-charge-for-interior-design) and [trade accounts and designer discounts](/interiors/trade-accounts-and-designer-discounts).
