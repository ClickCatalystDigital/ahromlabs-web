title: "Schedule of values"
answer: >
  The breakdown of a contract sum into lines of work, each with its scheduled value, against which every pay application reports the percentage complete; it becomes the G703 continuation sheet.
domain: [operations]
order: 23
published: 2026-11-05
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because every pay application is measured against it: if the lines are too coarse, the owner can't check progress and payments stall; if they don't add up to the contract sum, every bill is questioned. Approved change orders are added to it as new lines rather than folded into old ones.

How it drives each pay application is in [pay applications (AIA G702 and G703)](/construction/pay-applications); how a change becomes a new line is in [change orders](/construction/change-orders).
