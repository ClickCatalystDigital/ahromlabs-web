title: "Variation order"
answer: >
  A record of a change after the BOQ is agreed: what was added, omitted or substituted, at what rate, and its
  effect on time and payment, signed before the changed work is done.
domain: [operations]
order: 14
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because once a client accepts a BOQ-based quotation, every later change is a variation, and an unwritten one is an argument at the final bill. Pricing it from the BOQ's own rates, as CPWD's contract conditions do, keeps it short: a listed item at the agreed rate, a new item from a rate analysis.

How a changed item and a new item are priced under CPWD's contract conditions is in [variation orders in interior work](/boq/variation-orders); the US equivalent is a [change order](/construction/change-orders).
