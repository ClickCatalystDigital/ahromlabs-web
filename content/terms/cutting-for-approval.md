title: "CFA (cutting for approval)"
answer: >
  A small piece cut from the actual dye lot of a fabric a designer has ordered, sent for approval before the vendor ships or uses it.
domain: [operations]
order: 27
published: 2026-11-07

---

It matters because dye lots vary: the sample in the book and the bolt in the warehouse can differ enough to spoil a room. An order waiting on a CFA is held until someone approves it, so a procurement system needs the date it was sent and approved beside the order, or it sits unnoticed.

See [interior design purchase orders and procurement](/interiors/purchase-orders-and-procurement).
