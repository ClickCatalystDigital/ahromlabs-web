title: "Retainage"
answer: >
  A share of each progress payment, often 5–10% in US contracts, that the owner holds back until the work is complete, to secure completion and corrections; called retention or security deposit in India.
domain: [operations]
order: 19
published: 2026-11-05

It matters because it is often a contractor's profit on the job, held until completion. Several US states cap it at 5%, including California and New York on private jobs, and set a deadline to release it; on federal jobs it is withheld only for unsatisfactory progress. On CPWD works in India a security deposit of 2.5% is deducted from each running bill.

How it is calculated, capped by state law and released is in [retainage in construction](/construction/retainage); the Indian deductions are in [RA bills](/construction/ra-bills).
