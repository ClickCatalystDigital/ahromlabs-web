title: "Manufacturer's Data Report"
answer: >
  The ASME form that records a stamped boiler or vessel as built, such as U-1A or P-2: design conditions,
  materials, thicknesses, joints, examination and test pressure, signed by the manufacturer and the Authorized
  Inspector.
domain: [pressure]

---

It is the document a buyer, an inspector and every later repair rely on. ASME publishes the forms free; since 1 January 2026 items built to the 2025 Code must be on the 2025 forms, and a report sent to the National Board on paper must match the Code's form exactly. A manufacturer authorized to register files it within 30 days of the Inspector's certification (NB-264).

Which form applies and what goes in it are in [data reports](/asme/data-reports). Assembling the file behind it as the job runs, not at the end, is what [document extraction](/services/document-extraction) and a [boiler and vessel shop's system](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing) are for.
