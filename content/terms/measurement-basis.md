title: "Measurement basis"
answer: >
  The rule a BOQ line is measured by: square metres, running metres or numbers, and what is deducted. Floors,
  ceilings, paint and woodwork follow IS 1200; modular kitchens and wardrobes have no standard, so the line must
  state its basis.

It matters because the same wardrobe can be 60 square feet or 8 running feet, so a line without its basis can't be compared between quotes. IS 1200 sets the basis for floors, ceilings, paint and woodwork, including what is deducted for openings; for modular kitchens and wardrobes the trade uses several bases, so the line must say which.

The rules, trade by trade, are in [how interior work is measured](/boq/measurement-rules); writing them into each line is in [how to make an interior BOQ](/boq/how-to-make-an-interior-boq).
