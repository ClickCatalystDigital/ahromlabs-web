title: "Measurement basis"
answer: >
  The rule a BOQ line is measured by: square metres, running metres or numbers, and what is deducted. Floors,
  ceilings, paint and woodwork follow IS 1200; modular kitchens and wardrobes have no standard, so the line must
  state its basis.
domain: [operations]
order: 15
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

The same wardrobe can be 60 square feet or 8 running feet, so a line without its basis can't be compared between quotes. The rules, trade by trade, are in [how interior work is measured](/boq/measurement-rules).
