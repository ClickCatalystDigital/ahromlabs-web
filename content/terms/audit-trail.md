title: "Audit trail (edit log)"
answer: >
  A record, kept by accounting software for every transaction, of each change made to the books and the date it was made, which the user cannot switch off.
domain: [operations]
order: 1
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

Indian companies that keep their books in accounting software must use software with this feature for financial years from 1 April 2023, under the proviso to rule 3(1) of the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014, and their auditors report on it.

In an ERP it means more than a history tab: the old value, the new value, who changed it and when, for every entry, including those migrated from an older system. See the [audit-trail requirement](/erp/requirements#accounts-audit-trail) and [GST, TDS and statutory compliance](/services/compliance-accounting).
