title: "Diagnostic analytics"
answer: >
  Analytics that answers why something happened, by comparing a figure with a plan, a norm, a previous period or a rule.
domain: [truth]
order: 25
published: 2026-11-14
updated: 2026-11-14
---

Examples: material used on a site compared with what the BOQ allows for the work done; billing this month against last month; customers silent for longer than their usual gap between orders. The comparison and its threshold should be stated with the answer.

See [the four levels of analytics](/ai-assistant/analytics-levels).
