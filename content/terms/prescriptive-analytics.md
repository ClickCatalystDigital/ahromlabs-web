title: "Prescriptive analytics"
answer: >
  Analytics that answers what to do: a ranked recommendation with the reason and the records behind it, such as which purchase to bring forward or which delay to deal with first.
domain: [truth]
order: 27
published: 2026-11-14
updated: 2026-11-14
---

It is the hardest level, because a wrong recommendation spends money. It should show the records and assumptions it used and how sure it is, and leave the decision to a person. In our assistants it is planned, and the planned questions are labelled as such.

See [the four levels of analytics](/ai-assistant/analytics-levels) and [market signals](/ai-assistant/market-signals).
