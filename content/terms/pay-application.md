answer: >
  A US contractor's periodic request for payment, usually on AIA forms G702 and G703: the value of work completed and materials stored to date, less retainage and earlier payments, certified by the architect.

It matters because it is how a US contractor gets paid: each line of the schedule of values with work completed before and this period, materials stored, retainage held back and the amount now due, certified by the architect or owner before payment. AIA's G702 is the one-page summary and G703 the continuation sheet; an approved change order is billed as its own line.

A line-by-line worked example is in [pay applications (AIA G702 and G703)](/construction/pay-applications); the retention it deducts is in [retainage](/construction/retainage) and the waivers that travel with it in [lien waivers](/construction/lien-waivers).
