answer: >
  Checking each supplier bill against its purchase order and the goods actually received before it is approved for payment.
domain: [operations]
order: 4
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because paying a bill on the bill alone is how a short supply or a quietly raised rate gets paid in full. Differences in quantity or rate between the purchase order, the goods received and the bill are flagged for a person before payment; a system should match only when the pairing is unambiguous and leave the rest to someone.

See the [three-way match requirement](/erp/requirements#purchase-three-way-match), the pattern [auto-match only when mutually unique](/patterns/auto-match-only-when-mutually-unique) and the note [suppliers quote on a link](/notes/suppliers-quote-on-a-link).
