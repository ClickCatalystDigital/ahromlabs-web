title: "Reverse charge"
answer: >
  A GST arrangement where the buyer, not the supplier, pays the tax on certain purchases.
domain: [operations]
order: 9
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

Because no supplier invoice shows the tax, an ERP must mark these purchases and compute the liability for the return. See the [reverse-charge requirement](/erp/requirements#accounts-reverse-charge).
