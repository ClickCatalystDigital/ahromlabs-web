title: "Nonconformance report (NCR)"
answer: >
  The record of anything that fails a requirement (the Code, a drawing, a purchase order), from discovery through
  disposition, repair, re-inspection and close. It holds the item and the steps that depend on it until it is closed.
domain: [pressure]
order: 16
published: 2026-11-14
updated: 2026-11-14
---

Dispositions are rework, repair, use as is, reject, or return to the supplier; on a Code item use as is is only possible when the item still meets the Code. A weld repair is re-examined by the same method, and the failed result stays on the record. See [NCRs in an ASME shop](/asme/nonconformance-report) and the [hold points](/systems/hold-point) an open NCR keeps closed.
