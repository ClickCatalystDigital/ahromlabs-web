title: "TDS on purchase of goods"
answer: >
  Tax a buyer deducts when paying a seller for goods beyond ₹50 lakh in a year, where the buyer's turnover exceeded ₹10 crore in the preceding year.
domain: [operations]
order: 8
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-07
---

It matters because the ERP has to track each seller's running total for the year and start deducting on the bill that crosses ₹50 lakh, which a person checking bills one by one can't do. Under the Income-tax Act, 2025 it is row 8(ii) of the table in section 393(1), formerly section 194Q of the 1961 Act, in force from 1 April 2026.

See [ERP compliance in India](/erp/compliance) and the [TDS requirement](/erp/requirements#accounts-tds-purchases); confirm your position with a CA. The [compliance accounting service](/services/compliance-accounting) builds the tracking.
