title: "Bill of quantities (BOQ)"
answer: >
  A list of every item of work with its description, unit and quantity, measured from the drawings before
  anyone prices it, so contractors fill in rates and their quotes can be compared line by line.
domain: [operations]
order: 13
published: 2026-10-25

---

It matters because two quotes are only comparable when they price the same lines: a BOQ fixes the items, units and quantities before anyone prices them, so a low quote can't hide by leaving work out. It is not an estimate (an expected cost), a quotation (a priced offer) or a bill of materials (the parts of one product).

How to write one for interior work, with a free checker and a 41-item library, is in the [interior BOQ guide](/boq/what-is-a-boq); how each trade is measured is in [measurement rules](/boq/measurement-rules), and how a firm quotes from it in [ERP for interior design firms](/erp/interior-design-firms).
