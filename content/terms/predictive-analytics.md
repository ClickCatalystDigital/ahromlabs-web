  Analytics that answers what is likely to happen, such as which items will run out in 30 days or whether a site will finish over budget.

A prediction a business can rely on states its rule and the history it used, gives the same answer when asked twice, and can be checked when the date arrives. Our assistants give projections from stated rules (days of stock cover, projected site margin); the AI model never produces the forecast.
