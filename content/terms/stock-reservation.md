answer: >
  Setting physical stock aside against a specific customer order line, so the same unit can't be promised to two customers.
domain: [operations]
order: 5
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-11-02
---

In the LS Technologies system, whole reels are reserved per customer PO line, oldest first, and shipping a reserved reel elsewhere needs a manager's override. See the [reservation requirement](/erp/requirements#stores-reservations) and [QR inventory and order reservations](/services/inventory-and-reservations). Accounting software usually has no reservations: see [QuickBooks Online inventory for manufacturers](/manufacturing/quickbooks-online-inventory).
