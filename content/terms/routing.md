answer: >
  The ordered list of operations that makes a part (for example cut, bend, weld, inspect), with the machine or work center, setup time and run time for each.

A [bill of materials](/systems#bill-of-materials) says what a part is made of; the routing says how. It is what turns a quote into a schedule and a job cost: the same operations and times price the job, place it on machines and become the estimate that actual time is compared against. No QuickBooks edition models routings; see [does QuickBooks work for manufacturing](/manufacturing/quickbooks-for-manufacturing) and [production scheduling software](/manufacturing/production-scheduling-software).
