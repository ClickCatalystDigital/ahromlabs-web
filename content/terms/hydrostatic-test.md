title: "Hydrostatic test"
answer: >
  The pressure test of a finished boiler or vessel filled with water, held above its MAWP and inspected for
  leaks: at least 1.3 × MAWP (times a stress ratio) for an ASME Section VIII Division 1 vessel, 1.5 × MAWP for a
  Section I boiler.
