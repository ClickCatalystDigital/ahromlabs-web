title: "Heat number"
answer: >
  The number a steel mill gives to one melt (heat) of steel, marked on the material and printed on its test
  certificate. It is the link between a piece of plate or pipe and the certificate that proves what it is.
domain: [pressure]
order: 11
published: 2026-11-01

---

It matters because it is the only link between a cut piece and the certificate that proves what it is. When plate is cut, the number has to be transferred to every piece, and to the offcut that goes back to stock, or the piece can't be used on Code or IBR work. An Inspector checks material against its certificate by heat number.

Keeping it on every cut piece is the core of [material traceability](/systems/material-traceability); see [material test reports](/asme/mtr), [plate remnants back into stock](/notes/plate-remnants-back-into-stock) and the [material traceability service](/services/material-traceability).
