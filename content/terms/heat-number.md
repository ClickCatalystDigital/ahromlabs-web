title: "Heat number"
answer: >
  The number a steel mill gives to one melt (heat) of steel, marked on the material and printed on its test
  certificate. It is the link between a piece of plate or pipe and the certificate that proves what it is.
domain: [pressure]
order: 11
published: 2026-11-01
updated: 2026-11-01
---

Keeping it on every cut piece is the core of [material traceability](/systems/material-traceability); see [material test reports](/asme/mtr).
