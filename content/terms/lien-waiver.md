title: "Lien waiver"
answer: >
  A signed release in which a contractor, subcontractor or supplier gives up its right to file a mechanics lien for the amount paid; conditional waivers take effect only once the payment clears, unconditional ones at signing.
domain: [operations]
order: 21

It matters because owners and lenders ask for one with every payment, and the wrong kind or wording can give up rights for money not yet received. A conditional waiver takes effect only once the payment reaches the signer; an unconditional one when signed. Several states, including Arizona, California, Florida, Texas and Wyoming, set the wording by statute, and a waiver that doesn't follow the statutory form can be unenforceable.

The four kinds, and the states that prescribe their wording, are in [lien waivers explained](/construction/lien-waivers); filing deadlines are in [mechanics lien deadlines](/construction/mechanics-lien-deadlines).
