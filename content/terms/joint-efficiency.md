title: "Joint efficiency"
answer: >
  The factor (E) applied to a welded joint in ASME pressure vessel thickness formulas, set by the joint type and
  how much it is radiographed: 1.0, 0.85 or 0.70 for a double-welded butt joint with full, spot or no
  radiography.
