title: "Joint efficiency"
answer: >
  The factor (E) applied to a welded joint in ASME pressure vessel thickness formulas, set by the joint type and
  how much it is radiographed: 1.0, 0.85 or 0.70 for a double-welded butt joint with full, spot or no
  radiography.

It matters to a buyer because it sets wall thickness, and so steel cost: the same 48 in shell at 150 psi needs 0.307 in with full radiography of its long seams and 0.385 in with none. Division 1 makes full radiography mandatory in some cases, such as lethal service and unfired steam boilers above 50 psi (UW-11), and otherwise leaves the choice to the designer.

The values and the worked example are in [joint efficiency and radiography](/asme/joint-efficiency); the [shell calculator](/asme/shell-thickness) takes a separate efficiency for the circumferential seams. A shop's weld map records each seam's category, type and radiography; see [ERP for boiler and pressure vessel shops](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing).
