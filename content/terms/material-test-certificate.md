title: "Material test certificate (MTC)"
answer: >
  The steel maker's certificate for a plate or tube: its material and test results, identified by certificate,
  cast and plate number. Under IBR 2026, the material certificates go with a boiler's registration (Form V Part A).
domain: [operations]
order: 16
published: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---

One certificate often covers parts of several boilers, so a system that keeps each one once and links it to every part avoids retyping and mismatches; see [the IBR statutory folder from a BOM](/notes/ibr-statutory-folder-from-bom) and [Form V Part A](/ibr/forms/form-v-part-a).
