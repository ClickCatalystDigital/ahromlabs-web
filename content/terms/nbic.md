title: "NBIC (National Board Inspection Code)"
answer: >
  The National Board's code for boilers and pressure vessels after they're built: installation, inservice
  inspection, repairs and alterations, and pressure relief devices. Repairs under it are done by holders of the
  R stamp.
domain: [pressure]
order: 14
published: 2026-11-01

---

It matters after the sale: every repair, alteration and relief-valve repair to a boiler or vessel in service is done and recorded under it, by a holder of the National Board's R, VR or NR certificate, on forms such as R-1 (repair) and R-2 (alteration). The National Board says the NBIC is adopted into law by most US and Canadian jurisdictions; it is republished every two years, the current edition being 2025.

See [National Board registration and the R stamp](/asme/national-board-registration) and [US state boiler laws](/asme/state-boiler-laws).
