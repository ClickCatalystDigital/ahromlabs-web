It matters because unapproved changes are the most common reason a contractor isn't paid for work done. AIA's form is G701, and a construction change directive (G714) orders changed work before the price is agreed; federal jobs give 30 days to assert an adjustment. Bill a change only once approved, as its own line on the pay application.

How change orders are priced, approved and billed is in [change orders in construction](/construction/change-orders); the Indian equivalent is a [variation order](/systems/variation-order).
