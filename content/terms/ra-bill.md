title: "RA bill (running account bill)"
answer: >
  An interim bill a contractor raises during the work in India, for the quantity measured so far less what earlier bills already claimed, with deductions such as security deposit, advances recovered and tax deducted at source shown on the bill.
domain: [operations]
order: 18
published: 2026-11-05

---

It matters because it is how an Indian contractor is paid while the work goes on, and its deductions decide the cash that arrives: security deposit (2.5% of each bill on CPWD works), TDS, GST TDS on government work, and recovery of any mobilisation or secured advance. Each bill is drawn from the measurement book, to date less earlier bills, and the final bill closes the contract.

How one is built, with previous, this-bill and up-to-date quantities, is in [RA bills: how a running account bill works](/construction/ra-bills); the tax side is in [GST on works contracts](/construction/gst-on-works-contracts) and [TDS on contractor payments](/construction/tds-on-contractor-payments).
