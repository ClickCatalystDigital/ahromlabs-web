## One mark, many designators

Since the early 2010s there has been one **ASME Certification Mark** (the cloverleaf), stamped together with a **designator**: the letters that say which section and which kind of work the item was built to. People still say "U stamp" for the mark with the U designator, and this guide does too.

## ASME designators

ASME's certification page lists these, with how long each certificate lasts and whether it needs an agreement with an Authorized Inspection Agency (from ASME's informational handbook):

| Section | Designator | For | Valid | Inspection agency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | S | Power boilers | 3 years | Yes |
| I | A | Assembly of power boilers | 3 years | Yes |
| I | E | Electric boilers | 3 years | Yes |
| I | M | Miniature boilers | 3 years | Yes |
| I | PP | Pressure piping (boiler external piping) | 3 years | Yes |
| IV | H | Heating boilers (not cast iron or aluminum) | 3 years | Yes |
| IV | H | Cast iron and cast aluminum heating boilers | 1 year | No |
| IV | HLW | Lined potable water heaters | 3 years | Yes |
| VIII-1 | U | Pressure vessels | 3 years | Yes |
| VIII-1 | UM | Miniature pressure vessels (needs a U or S too) | 1 year | Through the U or S |
| VIII-2 | U2 | Pressure vessels, alternative rules | 3 years | Yes |
| VIII-3 | U3 | High-pressure vessels | 3 years | Yes |
| X | RP | Fiber-reinforced plastic vessels | 3 years | Yes |
| XII | T | Transport tanks | 3 years | Yes |
| I, IV, VIII-1, VIII-2, XII | PRT | Parts, without design responsibility | 3 years | Yes |
| XIII | V, HV, UV, UD, UV3, UD3, TV, TD | Relief valves and devices | 3 years | No |

PRT is for parts makers who build to someone else's design: the certificate holder who buys the part takes design responsibility. Relief device certificates have their own review route; see [Section XIII](/asme/section-xiii).

## National Board marks

These are not ASME's; they come from the National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors:

- **NB**: the item's data report is registered with the National Board. Many US jurisdictions require it; see [National Board registration](/asme/national-board-registration).
- **R**: repairs and alterations to boilers and pressure vessels in service, under the NBIC.
- **VR**: repair of pressure relief valves.
- **NR**: repairs and replacements at nuclear facilities.
- **T/O**: testing of pressure relief valves.

## What the stamp means to a buyer

A stamp says the item was built under an accepted quality system and inspected by an [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector), and that a [data report](/asme/data-reports) exists for it. It says nothing about whether the design suits your process; that is set by the design conditions you specify. To get a stamp, see [how to get the U stamp](/asme/u-stamp).
