## Side by side

| | B31.1 Power Piping | B31.3 Process Piping |
|---|---|---|
| Typical plants | Power stations, central heating and cooling plants, industrial steam systems | Refineries, chemical, petrochemical, pharmaceutical, food, gas processing |
| Special scope | Boiler external piping (BEP) | Fluid service categories: Normal, Category D, Category M, High Pressure, High Purity |
| Hazard approach | One set of rules, conservative | Rules scale with the fluid's hazard |
| Hydrostatic test | 1.5 × design pressure | 1.5 × design pressure, adjusted by the stress ratio at temperature |
| Welding qualification | Section IX | Section IX (with B31.3's own additions) |
| Stamp | BEP by a Section I certificate holder; otherwise none | None |

## Boiler external piping

The piping from a power boiler out to the first stop valve and beyond, as Section I defines it, is **boiler external piping**. It is designed to B31.1 but is part of the Section I boiler: built by a holder of a Section I certificate (S, A or PP) and inspected by the [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector). See [Section I vs Section IV](/asme/section-i-vs-iv).

## B31.3 fluid service categories

- **Normal**: most services.
- **Category D**: non-flammable, non-toxic fluids at low pressure and moderate temperature; lighter rules.
- **Category M**: fluids where a single small exposure can cause serious harm; stricter rules.
- **High Pressure** and **High Purity**: chapters of their own.

The owner is responsible for assigning the category.

## What carries over from the BPVC

- Welding procedures and welders are qualified to [Section IX](/asme/section-ix); a shop's [WPS and PQR](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq) can serve both its vessel and piping work where the variables fit.
- NDE methods come from [Section V](/asme/section-v); the piping code sets how much and the acceptance criteria.
- Materials are mostly ASME or ASTM specifications; see [Section II](/asme/section-ii).

Piping test pressures follow the same logic as vessels: see the [hydrostatic test](/asme/hydrostatic-test) guide.
