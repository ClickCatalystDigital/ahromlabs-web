## The test positions

| Position | Plate | Pipe |
|---|---|---|
| 1G | Flat | Pipe horizontal, rolled while welding |
| 2G | Horizontal (vertical plate, horizontal weld) | Pipe vertical, weld runs horizontally around it |
| 3G | Vertical | (plate only) |
| 4G | Overhead | (plate only) |
| 5G | – | Pipe horizontal and fixed: the welder goes flat, vertical and overhead round it |
| 6G | – | Pipe fixed at 45°: every position in one weld |

F (1F to 5F) positions are the fillet weld equivalents.

## What a test qualifies

Section IX's position table (QW-461.9) sets what each test covers. The headline cases:

- **6G pipe**: groove welds in all positions, plate and pipe, and fillets in all positions.
- **2G plus 5G pipe** (two tests): the same as 6G.
- **3G and 4G plate**: plate in all positions, but pipe only above a large diameter, which is why pipe shops test on pipe.
- **1G**: flat only.

Diameter limits also apply (QW-452.3): a small-bore test coupon qualifies down to roughly its own size, and a coupon of 2⅞ in outside diameter or more qualifies that diameter and up. Thickness limits come from the deposited weld metal on the coupon.

## How the test is judged

- **Bend tests** (QW-302): coupons cut from the test weld and bent; cracks or openings beyond the limit fail.
- **Radiography or ultrasonic examination** (QW-302, QW-304): allowed instead of bends for most processes and materials, and much faster, but not for short-circuiting GMAW, which can hide lack of fusion.

## Welders and welding operators

A **welder** holds and guides the torch or electrode. A **welding operator** runs machine or automatic welding equipment, and has a separate, simpler qualification. The WPQ form records which.

## Keeping it

A welder's qualification doesn't expire on a calendar date: it lapses after six months without welding in the process. See [welder continuity](/asme/welder-continuity), and for the variables that force a retest, [essential variables](/asme/essential-variables).

In India, a boiler welder needs an IBR welder certificate as well; see [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).
