## What does a 6G welding test qualify for?

A 6G test, on pipe fixed at 45°, qualifies the welder for groove welds in all positions on both plate and pipe, and
for fillet welds in all positions, within the diameter and thickness ranges the test coupon earns. Section IX's
position table (QW-461.9) sets what each test position covers. It is the test pipe shops use, because one coupon
covers every position. Section IX is ASME's paid Code; this guide cites its paragraphs, and your copy of the
[2025 Edition](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/bpvc-ix-bpvc-section-ix-welding-brazing-fusing-qualifications) is the authority.

## What do 1G to 6G mean?

They are the test positions for groove welds; the F positions (1F to 5F) are the fillet weld equivalents.

| Position | Plate | Pipe |
|---|---|---|
| 1G | Flat | Pipe horizontal, rolled while welding |
| 2G | Horizontal (vertical plate, horizontal weld) | Pipe vertical, weld runs horizontally around it |
| 3G | Vertical | (plate only) |
| 4G | Overhead | (plate only) |
| 5G | – | Pipe horizontal and fixed: the welder goes flat, vertical and overhead round it |
| 6G | – | Pipe fixed at 45°: every position in one weld |

## Does 2G plus 5G equal 6G?

Yes. Passing both a 2G and a 5G pipe test qualifies the same positions as a single 6G test, which is how some shops
qualify welders who find 6G hard to pass in one go.

## Do plate tests qualify a welder for pipe?

Only in part. A plate test qualifies plate in the positions tested, but on pipe it qualifies only large diameters
(over 24 in, 610 mm, outside diameter) in those positions, with smaller pipe limited as QW-461.9 sets out. That is why
pipe shops test on pipe.

## What pipe diameter does my test qualify?

The coupon's outside diameter sets the smallest pipe a welder may weld (QW-452.3): a coupon under 1 in (25 mm)
qualifies its own size and up; 1 in to under 2⅞ in (73 mm) qualifies 1 in and up; 2⅞ in and over qualifies 2⅞ in and
up. Thickness limits come from the deposited weld metal thickness on the coupon (QW-452.1).

## How is a welder's test judged?

Either by bend tests or by volumetric examination (QW-302):

- **Bend tests**: coupons cut from the test weld and bent around a mandrel; cracks or open defects beyond the limit fail.
- **Radiography or ultrasonic examination** (QW-304): allowed instead of bends for most processes and materials, and much faster. Gas metal arc welding in short-circuit transfer (GMAW-S), which can hide lack of fusion, can't be qualified by radiography, but ASME's published interpretation IX-16-28 (2015 Edition) says a GMAW-S test weld may be accepted by ultrasonic examination under QW-304 and QW-191.2. Otherwise those welders are bend-tested, and a lapsed GMAW-S qualification can't be renewed by radiographing a production weld (interpretation IX-04-13). Check both against your 2025 Section IX.

## What is the difference between a welder and a welding operator?

A welder holds and guides the electrode or torch; a welding operator sets up and runs machine or automatic welding
equipment. They have separate qualifications with their own essential variables (QW-350 for welders, QW-360 for
operators), and ASME's suggested forms are separate: QW-484A for a welder's WPQ and QW-484B for a welding operator's.

## What changes force a welder to retest?

A change in one of the welder's essential variables beyond the WPQ: the process, a position not covered, backing
(welding without backing when qualified only with it), the filler F-number outside what QW-433 allows, or diameter
and thickness outside the earned range. See [essential variables](/asme/essential-variables) and
[P-numbers and F-numbers](/asme/p-numbers).

## Does an ASME welder qualification expire?

Not on a calendar date. It lapses when the welder hasn't welded with that process for six months (QW-322.1(a)), and
can be revoked sooner if there is a specific reason to question their work. See
[welder continuity](/asme/welder-continuity), which has a free checker.

## Is a WPQ valid at another employer?

Not automatically. Section IX makes each organization responsible for the qualifications it uses (Part QG), so a new
employer either qualifies the welder again or accepts the earlier qualification only as Section IX allows. Ask for the
welder's WPQ and continuity record, and check them with your Authorized Inspector.

## Does a boiler welder in India need anything else?

Yes: in India a welder on boilers under the Indian Boiler Regulations also needs an IBR welder certificate. See
[ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).
