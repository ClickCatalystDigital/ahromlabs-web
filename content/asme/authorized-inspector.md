## The people and the organizations

- **Authorized Inspection Agency (AIA)**: an organization accredited by ASME to provide inspection for Code construction. Most are insurance companies that write boiler and machinery cover; some are state or provincial jurisdictions acting as their own agency.
- **Authorized Inspector (AI)**: the agency's employee who does the inspecting. They hold a National Board commission with the endorsement for new construction, earned through education, experience and an examination.
- **AI Supervisor**: the agency's senior inspector who oversees the AI and takes part in joint reviews and audits.

A shop can't hire an AI on its own; it signs an inspection agreement with an agency, which assigns the inspector. Most ASME certificates require that agreement before ASME will schedule a [joint review](/asme/u-stamp).

## What the Inspector does on a job

- Reviews the design calculations and drawings for Code compliance.
- Checks material against its [MTR](/asme/mtr) and its marking.
- Checks that every weld is made to a qualified [WPS](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq) by a qualified welder, and that welders are in [continuity](/asme/welder-continuity).
- Reviews NDE results and heat treatment records.
- Witnesses the [hydrostatic or pneumatic test](/asme/hydrostatic-test).
- Signs the [data report](/asme/data-reports) and authorizes the stamping.

The shop's quality control manual names the Inspector's hold points: the steps where work stops until the AI has seen it.

## The Inspector is not the shop's QC

The AI checks that the shop's system worked; they don't run it. If the shop's own inspection is missing, the AI reports it as a nonconformity rather than doing it for them.

## Repairs in service

Repairs and alterations to equipment already installed are inspected by a National Board commissioned inspector under the NBIC, with the repair company holding the National Board R stamp. See [National Board registration and the R stamp](/asme/national-board-registration).
