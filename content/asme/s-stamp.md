## What is the ASME S stamp?

S is ASME's Certificate of Authorization for power boilers under Section I of the Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code.
ASME lists it first among the Section I certificates, as "S - Power Boilers", followed by A (assembly), E (electric
boilers), M (miniature boilers) and [PP (pressure piping)](/asme/pp-stamp). A shop that holds S may build power
boilers, their parts and their boiler external piping, and apply the ASME Certification Mark with the S designator.

As with every ASME stamp, the separate code symbol stamps were replaced in 2013 by one Certification Mark; "S
stamp" is the name everyone still uses. See [ASME stamps](/asme/stamps) for the full list.

## Which boilers need the S stamp?

Section I covers power boilers: steam boilers that generate steam above 15 psi, high-temperature water boilers above
160 psi or above 250 °F, and the economizers, superheaters, reheaters and external piping that are part of them.
Typical work: watertube and firetube steam boilers for plants, heat recovery steam generators, waste heat boilers,
superheaters and economizers, and the pressure parts of process steam generators.

Low-pressure heating boilers (steam up to 15 psi, hot water up to 160 psi and 250 °F) are built to Section IV with
the H stamp instead. See [Section I vs Section IV](/asme/section-i-vs-iv).

## What is the difference between the S, A, E, M and PP stamps?

| Certificate | Scope |
|---|---|
| S | Power boilers: design and manufacture, parts and piping |
| A | Field assembly of power boilers from parts made by others |
| E | Electric boilers |
| M | Miniature boilers (small size and pressure limits in Section I) |
| [PP](/asme/pp-stamp) | Pressure piping for power boilers, mainly boiler external piping |

A boiler maker usually holds S, often with PP. An erection contractor that assembles boilers on site holds A. A shop
that only fabricates parts for others (headers, drums, tube panels) without design responsibility may hold the PRT
parts certificate instead.

## What does a shop need to get the S stamp?

- **An inspection agreement.** ASME's handbook: "the Applicant must have an inspection agreement in place with an
  ASME Accredited Authorized Inspection Agency (AIA)". Its table lists the S certificate as valid for 3 years with
  the agreement required. See [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector).
- **A quality control manual** for Section I work: design and calculations, drawings, material control by heat
  number, welding, examination, heat treatment, hydrostatic test, calibration, nonconformances and records. See the
  [QC manual](/asme/quality-control-manual).
- **The code books.** ASME lists S together with PP ("Pressure Piping and Power Boilers"): Section I, Section II
  Parts A, B, C and D, Section V, Section IX, B31.1 and CA-1.
- **Welding qualifications** to [Section IX](/asme/section-ix): procedures, welders and their
  [continuity](/asme/welder-continuity).
- **A demonstration item** for the joint review, built and inspected under the new system.

## How much does the S stamp cost?

ASME's price guide: "All other Certificates: $4,500.00 per certificate and Advance Deposit: $11,000.00", plus $400 per
Certification Mark stamp. The deposit goes toward the review. The inspection agency's contract, the code books and
the work of writing the manual and qualifying procedures come on top. The certificate is renewed every three years.

## Can an S holder add A and PP without another review?

Yes. ASME's FAQ: "A full ASME Joint Review is not required for: A holder of an ASME S Certificate who applies for an
additional A or PP Certificate(s) of Authorization." ASME asks the last review's team leader and the shop's inspection
agency to confirm in writing that the manual covers the added scope. A shop with only the U certificate needs a full
review to add S.

## Which data reports go with S work?

ASME's Section I forms include:

| Form | Use |
|---|---|
| P-2 | "Manufacturers' Data Report for All Types of Boilers Except Watertube and Electric" |
| P-3 | "Manufacturers' Data Report for Watertube Boilers, Superheaters, Waterwalls, and Economizers" |
| P-3A | "Engineering-Contractor Data Report for a Complete Boilers Unit" |
| P-4 | "Manufacturers' Partial Data Report", for parts made by one holder and used by another |
| P-4A | Fabricated piping (see the [PP stamp](/asme/pp-stamp)) |

The shop completes the report, the Authorized Inspector signs it, and in most states it is registered with the
National Board. See [data reports](/asme/data-reports) and
[National Board registration](/asme/national-board-registration).

## What records does an S shop keep per boiler?

The calculations and drawings with revisions, material by heat number with its [MTRs](/asme/mtr), a weld map with
the procedure and welder per seam, NDE and [PWHT](/asme/pwht) records, the
[hydrostatic test](/asme/hydrostatic-test), the [ITP](/asme/inspection-and-test-plan) with its hold points signed,
any [NCRs](/asme/nonconformance-report), partial data reports from suppliers, and the signed data report. Together
they make the [manufacturer's data book](/asme/data-book).
