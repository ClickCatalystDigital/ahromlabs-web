## What Section IX does, and what it doesn't

Section IX answers two questions: **can this procedure make a sound weld**, and **can this person make it**. It does that with test coupons and mechanical or volumetric tests, and with rules for how far a passed test stretches (which materials, thicknesses, positions and processes it covers).

It does not say which joints need welding, what type of joint, which welds are radiographed or when heat treatment is needed. That is the construction section's job: [Section VIII](/asme/section-viii), Section I, or a B31 piping code, which then sends you to Section IX for qualification.

## Its parts

| Part | Covers |
|---|---|
| QG | General requirements shared by all processes |
| QW | Welding: procedure qualification (QW-200), performance qualification (QW-300), the variables (QW-400) and standard welding procedure specifications (QW-500) |
| QB | Brazing |
| QF | Plastic fusing (polyethylene pipe) |

## The three documents

- **WPS** (welding procedure specification): the written instructions a welder follows, with ranges for each variable.
- **PQR** (procedure qualification record): the record of the test coupon that proves the WPS works: what was actually used, and the test results.
- **WPQ** (welder or welding operator performance qualification): the record that a person passed their test, and what it qualifies them for.

How the three link, and what goes in each, is in [WPS vs PQR vs WPQ](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq). Which changes need a new PQR is in [essential variables](/asme/essential-variables); which positions a welder's test covers is in [welder qualification](/asme/welder-qualification); and how a welder keeps their qualification alive is the [continuity rule](/asme/welder-continuity).

## Material grouping

Section IX groups base metals by **P-number** and filler metals by **F-number** and **A-number**, so one test covers a family of materials instead of one specification. See [P-numbers, F-numbers and A-numbers](/asme/p-numbers).

## The 2025 Edition

The Section IX changes in the 2025 Edition became mandatory for new qualifications on 1 January 2026, as the Welding Journal's summary of changes notes. Existing qualifications made to earlier editions stay valid; the [editions guide](/asme/editions) explains how that works.
