## Why it exists

Until 2021 each construction section carried its own relief device rules: Section VIII Division 1 in its UG-125 series of paragraphs, Section IV and Section I in theirs. The 2021 Edition gathered the device rules into one new section so that a valve maker works to one book. The construction sections kept the requirement to protect the equipment and point to Section XIII for the device. In older drawings and data sheets you'll still see "UG-125"; on new work the device side is Section XIII.

## The device stamps

| Designator | Device |
|---|---|
| V | Boiler pressure relief valves |
| HV | Heating boiler safety valves |
| UV | Pressure vessel pressure relief valves |
| UD | Pressure vessel nonreclosing devices (rupture disks and similar) |
| UV3, UD3 | High-pressure vessel devices (Division 3) |
| TV, TD | Transport tank devices |

Unlike a vessel certificate, these need no agreement with an inspection agency. Instead, the National Board appoints the reviewer, and the device's relieving capacity has to be certified by test at an accepted laboratory; the time limits for those tests are quoted below. See [ASME stamps](/asme/stamps) for the full list.

## What stays with the vessel designer

The construction section still decides:

- that the vessel or boiler needs protection, and from what (a blocked outlet, a fire, a failed control);
- the set pressure, normally not above the [MAWP](/asme/mawp);
- how far pressure may rise while the device relieves: in Division 1, generally 10% or 3 psi above MAWP for one device, 16% with several, 21% for fire.

## Repairs

Setting and repairing relief valves in service is not ASME Section XIII work. It is done under the National Board's VR program; see [National Board registration and the R stamp](/asme/national-board-registration).
