## Method in Section V, verdict in the construction section

A radiograph is taken the way Section V says: film or digital, source and geometry, image quality indicators, density. Whether the indication on it is acceptable is judged by the construction section: in Section VIII Division 1, for example, full radiography is judged under UW-51 and spot radiography under UW-52. That split is why an NDE procedure cites Section V and the report cites Section VIII.

## The main Articles

| Article | Method |
|---|---|
| 1 | General requirements |
| 2 | Radiographic examination (RT), film and digital |
| 4 | Ultrasonic examination (UT) of welds, including phased array and TOFD |
| 6 | Liquid penetrant examination (PT) |
| 7 | Magnetic particle examination (MT) |
| 9 | Visual examination (VT) |
| 10 | Leak testing |
| 23 | Material specifications for NDE (adopted ASTM standards) |

## Personnel

The construction sections require NDE personnel to be qualified and certified to a written practice. In US and Canadian shops that is usually the employer's written practice to ASNT's SNT-TC-1A, with Level I, II and III personnel. IBR 2026 accepts ASNT certificates alongside the Indian Society for Non-destructive Testing's; see [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).

## Where it meets the rest of the Code

- How much radiography a joint gets changes its **joint efficiency** and so its required thickness: see [joint efficiency and radiography](/asme/joint-efficiency).
- NDE reports go into the vessel's file and are listed on the [data report](/asme/data-reports) package.
- A welder can be qualified by radiography instead of bend tests for most processes; see [welder qualification](/asme/welder-qualification).
