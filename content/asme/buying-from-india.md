## Why buyers look at India

India has a large base of fabricators building boilers, pressure vessels and heat exchangers for refineries, power and process plants, many with ASME certificates for export work, and engineering and fabrication rates below US levels. The Code is the same wherever the shop is: a U stamp from a shop in Gujarat means what one from Texas does.

## What to check before ordering

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| The certificate covers the work | Designator (U, U2, S, PP), scope (shop or field), and that it is current; ask for a copy and confirm on ASME's certificate holder listing. See [ASME stamps](/asme/stamps) |
| Who the Authorized Inspection Agency is | The AIA named on their agreement; the [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector) signs your [data report](/asme/data-reports) |
| National Board registration | If your state requires NB registration (most do), the shop must be authorized to register. See [National Board registration](/asme/national-board-registration) |
| The edition | Name the Code edition in the order; see [editions](/asme/editions) |
| Your jurisdiction's own rules | Some states and every Canadian province add requirements; see [US and Canadian boiler laws](/asme/state-boiler-laws) |
| Materials | SA specifications with MTRs, and where the mill is. See [MTRs](/asme/mtr) |
| Documents | The full package in English: calculations, drawings, MTRs, WPS/PQR, welder records, NDE, PWHT charts, test record, data report |

## Boilers: the IBR side

For a boiler, the Indian Boiler Regulations add a step at home. Regulation 4 of IBR 2026 lets an Indian Inspecting Authority inspect a boiler meant for export, through its stages of construction, against a foreign code such as ASME, and certify it in Form I Part A. That certificate is for India's records; it doesn't replace the ASME stamp or the Authorized Inspector. See [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr) and [IBR Form I Part A](/ibr/forms/form-i-part-a).

## Managing the order

- **Inspection and test plan**: agree hold points (material review, fit-up, NDE, PWHT, hydrostatic test) and who witnesses each, yours and the AI's.
- **Document review before shipping**: get the data report package as a draft before the vessel ships, not with it.
- **Logistics**: oversized vessels need shipping planned early; the data report and nameplate must match what arrives.

## Records that travel well

A buyer overseas sees a shop through its documents. Shops that keep heat numbers, welders and NDE in one system send a complete package on the day the vessel is ready; that is the system we build.
