## What is National Board registration for a pressure vessel?

It is the filing of a stamped item's Manufacturer's Data Report with the National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel
Inspectors (NBBI), which keeps it as a permanent record; the nameplate then carries the NB mark and a National Board
number. The National Board describes registration as "the culmination of a three-step process": construction to the
ASME Code, inspection by a National Board commissioned inspector, and the documentation certifying compliance.

## Who is the National Board?

The body of the chief boiler inspectors of most US states and Canadian provinces and territories, and many large US
cities. ASME writes the construction code; the National Board commissions inspectors, keeps the registry of built
items, and writes the code for equipment once it's in service, the National Board Inspection Code (NBIC).

## Is National Board registration required?

In many jurisdictions, yes. ASME's handbook warns that registration "may be required by the legal authority of a
state or municipality of the United States or province of Canada". Pennsylvania, for example, requires an ASME boiler
or unfired pressure vessel to be stamped with "its National Board registration number" (34 Pa. Code §3a.21(b)), and
California requires new air tanks to be National Board registered. Check the jurisdiction for the place of
installation; see [US and Canadian boiler laws](/asme/state-boiler-laws).

## How does a manufacturer become authorized to register?

By meeting the National Board's criteria in NB-264 and applying online. The National Board says: "There is no charge
to become authorized to register, but there is a fee for each item registered." The manufacturer's quality control
manual then says how registration numbers are controlled, who submits the forms and how fast.

## How soon must a data report be registered?

Within 30 days of certification. NB-264 (Revision 19) says the time between the Inspector's certification and
submitting the registration forms "shall not exceed 30 days".

## How are data reports filed?

On paper (the original report, in the exact format of the Code's form) or through the National Board's Electronic
Data Transfer (EDT) system. Since 1 January 2026 items built to the 2025 Code must be on the 2025 forms; reports on
outdated forms are returned. See [data reports](/asme/data-reports).

## What does the NB mark on a nameplate mean?

That the item's data report is on file with the National Board. In the National Board's words, "The NB mark is used by
manufacturers to identify items registered with the National Board." For an owner, it means a lost data report can
always be replaced, which matters at every later inspection, repair or sale.

## What is the NBIC?

The National Board Inspection Code: the rules for pressure equipment after it is built, in four Parts: Part 1
Installation, Part 2 Inspection, Part 3 Repairs and Alterations, and Part 4 Pressure Relief Devices. The National Board
says the NBIC "is adopted into law by most US and Canadian jurisdictions", and it is published every two years; the
current edition is 2025.

## What changed in the 2025 NBIC?

Among hundreds of revisions, the National Board's summary says "Quality System" became "Quality Management System"
throughout, and new supplements were added for classifying repairs and alterations (Supplement 10) and for engineered
repairs and alterations (Supplement 11). Check the (25) markers in your copy for the details.

## What are the R, VR, NR and T/O stamps?

| Stamp | For |
|---|---|
| R | Repairs and alterations to boilers, pressure vessels and other pressure-retaining items |
| VR | Repair of pressure relief valves |
| NR | Repair and replacement of nuclear components |
| T/O | In-service testing of pressure relief valves |

## How does a company get the R stamp, and what does it cost?

By having an inspection agreement with an Authorized Inspection Agency, a written quality system that meets the
current NBIC and NB-415, the current NBIC itself and the construction code for its scope, then passing an on-site
review, which can be held together with an ASME joint review. The National Board's page gives the certificate fee as
$1,150 for three years, plus a one-time $150 for the loan of the steel R stamp for new holders.

## Which forms record a repair or alteration?

NBIC Form R-1, Report of Repair (NB-66), and Form R-2, Report of Alteration (NB-229), both available through EDT; R-3
reports parts fabricated by welding and R-4 is a supplementary sheet. For registered items, the R-1 or R-2 is filed
with the National Board too.

Our US boiler demo records each job's National Board number on its ASME record package and data report layout, with
the full record behind it. It doesn't file through EDT; a shop files the official form there or on paper. See
