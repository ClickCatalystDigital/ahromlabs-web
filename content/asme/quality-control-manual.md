## What it is for

The manual is the written description of the shop's quality control system. ASME doesn't certify a product; it certifies that the shop has a system that will keep producing Code items, and the manual is how that system is described. At the [joint review](/asme/u-stamp) the team reads the manual, then watches the shop do what it says.

## The elements

For Section VIII Division 1 the elements are listed in its quality control system appendix (Mandatory Appendix 10 in recent editions); Section I and the others have equivalents. In outline:

| Element | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| Authority and responsibility | Who is responsible for quality, and that they can stop work |
| Organization | An org chart showing the quality function's independence |
| Drawings, calculations and specifications | How they are prepared or received, checked, approved, revised and issued |
| Material control | Receiving, checking against the [MTR](/asme/mtr), marking and [traceability](/asme/mtr) through cutting |
| Examination and inspection program | What is checked, when, and the Inspector's hold points |
| Correction of nonconformities | How a nonconformity is recorded, decided and closed |
| Welding | [WPS, PQR and WPQ](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq) control, welder identification, [continuity](/asme/welder-continuity) |
| Nondestructive examination | Procedures to [Section V](/asme/section-v), personnel qualification |
| Heat treatment | Procedures and records, including [PWHT](/asme/pwht) charts |
| Calibration | Gauges and test equipment, intervals and records |
| Records retention | Which records are kept, where, for how long |
| Sample forms | Every form the manual refers to |
| Inspection of vessels and parts | How the Authorized Inspector is given access and information |
| Relief valves | How they are inspected, where in scope |

ASME also gives applicants a checklist to cross-reference each element to the manual's paragraphs; the completed checklist goes to the review team.

## What makes a manual pass

- **It describes this shop.** A bought template with another company's job titles and forms fails the moment the team asks someone to show a step.
- **Every form exists and is used.** The forms in the manual are the forms on the shop floor.
- **Records join up.** A reviewer picks a weld on the demonstration item and follows it to the welder, their qualification, the WPS, the PQR, the filler metal and the NDE report. Each link has to be there.

That last test is the one most shops find hardest on paper and easiest in software: a record per weld, heat number and welder, linked once, rather than files cross-referenced by hand.
