## What should an ASME quality control manual include?

Every element of the quality control system the Code lists for the shop's scope, described as the shop actually works.
ASME's handbook puts it plainly: the applicant "must address each element of the QCS in the Code", but "need only
demonstrate those elements within the intended scope of activities that apply to their program". For Section VIII
Division 1 the elements are listed in its quality control system appendix (Mandatory Appendix 10); Section I and the
other construction sections have equivalents. In outline:

| Element | What the manual says |
|---|---|
| Authority and responsibility | Who is responsible for quality, and that they can stop work |
| Organization | An org chart showing the quality function's independence |
| Drawings, calculations and specifications | How they are prepared or received, checked, approved, revised and issued |
| Material control | Receiving, checking against the [MTR](/asme/mtr), marking and traceability through cutting |
| Examination and inspection program | What is checked, when, and the Inspector's hold points |
| Correction of nonconformities | How a nonconformity is recorded, decided and closed |
| Welding | [WPS, PQR and WPQ](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq) control, welder identification, [continuity](/asme/welder-continuity) |
| Nondestructive examination | Procedures to [Section V](/asme/section-v), personnel qualification |
| Heat treatment | Procedures and records, including [PWHT](/asme/pwht) charts |
| Calibration | Gauges and test equipment, intervals and records |
| Records retention | Which records are kept, where, for how long |
| Sample forms | Every form the manual refers to |
| Inspection of vessels and parts | How the Authorized Inspector is given access and information |

## Does the manual have to follow ASME's format?

No. The handbook says "The Manual need not follow the format of this Guide." What it must do is cover every element
for the scope, and the shop must show where: ASME's review checklist (below) maps each element to the manual's own
paragraphs.

## Does the manual have to be in English?

Yes. The handbook requires that "The written description and documentation which support understanding of the
program must be in English." A shop in India or elsewhere may run the floor in another language, but the manual and
the documents the review team reads are in English.

## What is ASME's review checklist?

The "Guide for ASME Review Teams for Review of Applicants": a checklist of the quality control system's elements for
the certificate applied for. ASME has four: one for pressure relief devices, one for RP (Section X), one for cast iron
and cast aluminum H boilers, and a General checklist for A, M, PP, S, E, H, HLW, U, UM, U2, U3, PRT and T. The
applicant fills in, against each element, the paragraph of its manual that addresses it, and gives the completed
guide to the review team.

## What is CA-1 and does the manual need it?

CA-1 is ASME's Conformity Assessment Requirements standard, which ASME now lists among the required code books for
every boiler and pressure vessel certificate. Buy it with the Code sections for your scope and check the manual
against both; where the 2025 construction section and CA-1 place a quality system requirement is worth confirming with
your Authorized Inspection Agency before the review.

## Can I buy a quality control manual template?

You can, but it has to describe your shop, and ASME won't write it for you: its FAQ says "ASME does not provide
consulting services to develop a Quality Control System". A bought template with another company's job titles and
forms fails the moment the team asks someone to show a step. Every form in the manual should be the form used on the
shop floor.

## How does the joint review test the manual?

It reads the manual, then watches the shop do what it says. The handbook describes an on-site evaluation of both the
written description and "a demonstration of implementation of the program", which may use "current work, a mock-up,
or a combination of the two". A reviewer typically picks a weld on the demonstration item and follows it to the welder,
their qualification and continuity, the WPS, the PQR, the filler metal and the NDE report; each link has to be there.
See [how to get the U stamp](/asme/u-stamp).

## Who checks the manual is followed after certification?

The [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector), on every job and at the agency's periodic audits, and the
next joint review at renewal. The manual's hold points are where the Inspector signs off, so the manual and the job's
inspection plan should name the same ones.

## How should records be kept so the manual works?

So that each link is recorded once: a record per weld, heat number and welder, joined to the job, rather than files
cross-referenced by hand. That is the test most shops find hardest on paper. Our US boiler demo keeps nonconformities
(with a QC verification step before an NCR can close), incoming inspection, hold points and the welding and
material records on each job, and prints the job's ASME record package from them. See
[material traceability](/services/material-traceability).
