## What is a Manufacturer's Data Report?

A Manufacturer's Data Report is the signed record of a stamped boiler, vessel or part as built: who made it and for
whom, design conditions, materials, thicknesses, joints, examinations, heat treatment and test pressure, certified
by the manufacturer and by the [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector). It is what the stamp points to:
the nameplate says the item is a Code item, and the data report says what that item is. Section VIII Division 1 sets
its data report rules in UG-120 (2025 Edition).

## Which data report form do I use?

The form follows the Code section and the kind of item. ASME publishes them free as fillable PDFs on its
[data report forms page](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/publications-information/asme-data-report-forms); the ones most shops use:

| Item | Form |
|---|---|
| Section VIII Div. 1 vessel | U-1, or U-1A (alternative form for single-chamber vessels) |
| Part of a Div. 1 vessel built by another shop | U-2 or U-2A (partial data report) |
| Plate heat exchanger | U-1P |
| Supplementary sheet, shell-and-tube heat exchanger | U-5 |
| Owner's design conditions | U-DR-1 (single chamber) or U-DR-2 (multi-chamber) |
| Section VIII Div. 2 vessel | A-1 (A-2 for parts) |
| Section VIII Div. 3 vessel | K-1 (K-2 for parts) |
| Section I boiler, except watertube and electric | P-2 (P-2A electric) |
| Section I watertube boilers, superheaters, waterwalls, economizers | P-3 |
| Section I parts and fabricated piping | P-4 and P-4A |
| Section IV boiler, except watertube and cast iron | H-2 (H-3 watertube, H-5 and H-5A cast iron and cast aluminum) |
| Section XIII relief valve or rupture disk | Certificates of conformance (UV-1, UD-1 and others) |
| B31.1 power piping | CC-1 and CC-2 certificates of compliance |

## What is the difference between U-1 and U-1A?

U-1A is the shorter alternative to U-1 for a single-chamber vessel. ASME's 2025 form is titled for "Single Chamber,
Completely Shop- or Field-Fabricated Vessels Only", so it now covers field-fabricated single-chamber vessels too.
Use U-1 for multi-chamber vessels such as heat exchangers (with the U-5 supplementary sheet for shell-and-tube units).

## What goes in a U-1A?

The report describes the vessel as built, not as quoted:

- manufacturer, purchaser and installation location, serial and National Board numbers;
- design pressure, [MAWP](/asme/mawp) at its coincident temperature, and the [MDMT](/asme/impact-testing);
- for each shell and head: material specification and grade, nominal thickness and corrosion allowance, inside diameter, length, [joint efficiency](/asme/joint-efficiency) and type, extent of radiography;
- nozzles and openings, with their reinforcement;
- heat treatment ([PWHT](/asme/pwht)) and impact testing;
- the [hydrostatic or pneumatic test pressure](/asme/hydrostatic-test);
- code cases used, and parts from other shops, with their U-2 reports attached;
- certification by the manufacturer and by the Authorized Inspector.

## Which edition of the forms must I use?

The 2025 forms, for anything built to the 2025 Code. The National Board's [registration page](https://www.nationalboard.org/Index.aspx?pageID=9) says the 2025
revisions to the ASME and NBIC report forms became mandatory on 1 January 2026, and that reports on outdated forms
are returned for correction. ASME's forms page stars the forms revised or new in the 2025 Edition (U-1, U-1A, U-2,
P-7, H-2 among them) and marks the metric versions such as P-2M and P-3M with a double star: "Form no longer included
in Code". See [ASME Code editions](/asme/editions).

## Can I make my own layout of the form?

Not for a report sent on paper to the National Board: it must be in the exact format and content of the form in the
Code and the NBIC, with National Board minimum font sizes, and a multi-page report must repeat its header and leave
space for the company representative and the Authorized Inspector to initial each page. ASME's own forms page warns
users to keep track of font and field size. A shop's software can still assemble the data and fill the official
form, or send it through the National Board's Electronic Data Transfer (EDT).

## Who signs the data report?

The manufacturer's representative certifies that the item meets the Code, and the Authorized Inspector certifies
that they inspected it; for a registered item the registration form bears the Inspector's signature and National
Board commission number. Without the Inspector's signature the item can't be stamped.

## How long must the shop keep the data report?

Division 1 requires the manufacturer to keep a copy for a period set in UG-120, or to register it with the National
Board, which keeps it permanently. Registration also means an owner can get a copy at any later inspection, repair or
sale. See [National Board registration](/asme/national-board-registration).

## How soon must a data report be filed with the National Board?

Within 30 days of certification. The National Board's criteria for registration (NB-264, Revision 19) require a
manufacturer authorized to register to submit registration forms no more than 30 days after the Inspector certifies
the item. Many US jurisdictions require registration before installation; see
[US and Canadian boiler laws](/asme/state-boiler-laws).

## What sits behind the one-page report?

The file the Inspector checked: calculations, drawings, [MTRs](/asme/mtr), weld map,
[WPS, PQR and welder qualifications](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq), NDE reports, heat treatment charts and the test record.
Assembling that file at the end of a job is where most shops lose days. Our US boiler demo builds it as the job runs
and prints it as one record package: a Form U-1A or P-2 layout (our own, carrying the form's fields, not ASME's form;
file the official form or use EDT), the weld map with welder stamps, WPS and PQR, QW-322 continuity, MTRs by heat
number, NDE, PWHT and the hydro test. See [ERP for boiler and pressure vessel shops](/industries/boiler-pressure-vessel-manufacturing).

## What is the Indian equivalent?

Under IBR 2026 the equivalents are the maker's certificate (Form IV Part A) and the Inspecting Authority's
certificate (Form I); see [IBR Form I Part A](/ibr/forms#form-i-part-a), [boiler registration documents](/ibr/registration-documents)
and [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).
