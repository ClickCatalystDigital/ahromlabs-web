## Which form

ASME publishes the forms free on its website as fillable PDFs. The ones most shops use:

| Item | Form |
|---|---|
| Section VIII Div. 1 vessel | U-1, or U-1A (alternative form for single-chamber vessels) |
| Part of a Div. 1 vessel built by another shop | U-2 or U-2A (partial data report) |
| Plate heat exchanger | U-1P |
| Supplementary sheet, shell-and-tube heat exchanger | U-5 |
| Section VIII Div. 2 vessel | A-1 (A-2 for parts) |
| Section VIII Div. 3 vessel | K-1 |
| Section I boiler, except watertube and electric | P-2 (P-2A electric) |
| Section I watertube boilers, superheaters, waterwalls, economizers | P-3 |
| Section I fabricated piping | P-4A |
| Section IV boiler, except watertube and cast | H-2 (H-3 watertube) |
| Section XIII relief valve or rupture disk | UV-1, HV-1, UD-1 or TV-1 (certificates of conformance) |

The 2025 forms became mandatory with the 2025 Code on 1 January 2026; see [ASME Code editions](/asme/editions).

## What goes in a U-1A

The report describes the vessel as built, not as quoted:

- manufacturer, purchaser and installation location, serial numbers;
- design pressure, [MAWP](/asme/mawp) and coincident temperature, and the [MDMT](/asme/impact-testing);
- for each shell and head: material specification and grade, nominal and corrosion thickness, inside diameter, length, [joint efficiency](/asme/joint-efficiency) and type, radiography;
- nozzles and openings, with their reinforcement;
- heat treatment ([PWHT](/asme/pwht)) and impact testing;
- the [hydrostatic or pneumatic test pressure](/asme/hydrostatic-test);
- code cases used, and parts from other shops (with their U-2 reports attached);
- certification by the manufacturer and by the [Authorized Inspector](/asme/authorized-inspector).

## The data report package

Behind the one-page report sits the file the Inspector checked: calculations, drawings, [MTRs](/asme/mtr), weld map, [WPS, PQR and welder qualifications](/asme/wps-pqr-wpq), NDE reports, heat treatment charts and the test record. Assembling that file at the end of a job is where most shops lose days; building it as the job runs is what the software we write does.

## Registration and keeping it

Section VIII Division 1 requires the manufacturer to keep a copy of the data report for a period set by the Code, or to register it with the National Board, which keeps it permanently. Many US jurisdictions require National Board registration for the item to be installed; the National Board asks for reports within 30 days of certification. See [National Board registration](/asme/national-board-registration).

## In India

The IBR equivalents are the maker's certificate (Form IV Part A) and the Inspecting Authority's certificate (Form I); see [IBR Form I Part A](/ibr/forms/form-i-part-a), [boiler registration documents](/ibr/registration-documents) and [ASME vs IBR 2026](/asme/asme-vs-ibr).
