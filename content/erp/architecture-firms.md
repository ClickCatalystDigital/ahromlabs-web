## How do architecture practices in India bill their fees?

Mostly as a percentage of the project cost or a lump sum, released in stages. A common pattern:

| Stage | What is delivered |
|---|---|
| Appointment | Retainer on signing |
| Concept design | Site study, concept options, client brief signed off |
| Preliminary design | Plans, sections, elevations, outline specifications |
| Approval drawings | Drawings for the local authority, submitted |
| Working drawings and tender | Construction drawings, specifications, BOQ, tender documents |
| Site supervision | Periodic visits, clarifications, certifying contractor bills |
| Completion | Completion drawings, occupancy support |

The share at each stage is agreed in the appointment letter. The Council of Architecture registers architects under
the Architects Act, 1972 and sets professional conduct rules; check its current guidance on fees and agreements.

## What taxes apply to an architect's invoice?

GST on architectural services, charged on each invoice at the applicable rate, with the practice's GSTIN, the client's
GSTIN for business clients, and the SAC code for the service. Business clients usually deduct TDS on professional
fees, which the practice claims against its income tax, so each invoice should be matched to the TDS credit shown in
the practice's tax statement. Your CA confirms rates and sections for your case.

## What else does a practice need to track?

- **Hours per project and stage**, to see which projects earn their fee.
- **Consultants**: structural, MEP and landscape fees, paid by the practice or by the client.
- **Drawings**: a register of every drawing issued, to whom, with its revision and date, so site works from the
  current set.
- **BOQs and tender documents** for the working drawing stage; see [what is a BOQ](/boq/what-is-a-boq) and the
  [interior quotation format](/boq/interior-quotation-format).
- **Contractor bills certified** during site supervision, with the measurement and the amount certified.

## Can you show a stage-wise fee schedule?

An example for a ₹12 lakh fee on a building project (shares and figures are made up; set yours in the appointment
letter):

| Stage | Share | Fee (₹) | GST at the applicable rate | Invoice when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Appointment retainer | 10% | 1,20,000 | Added on the invoice | Signing |
| Concept design | 10% | 1,20,000 | Added | Concept approved |
| Preliminary design | 15% | 1,80,000 | Added | Design frozen |
| Approval drawings | 15% | 1,80,000 | Added | Submitted to the authority |
| Working drawings and tender | 30% | 3,60,000 | Added | Issued for construction |
| Site supervision | 15% | 1,80,000 | Added | Spread over construction months |
| Completion | 5% | 60,000 | Added | Completion drawings handed over |
| **Total** | **100%** | **12,00,000** | | |

Each invoice then carries GST, and the client deducts TDS on payment; match each receipt to the invoice and the TDS
credit.

## How do you know if a project is earning its fee?

Compare the fee earned to date with the cost of the hours spent: if a project has earned 40% of its fee and used 70%
of the hours budgeted for the whole job, it will lose money unless the remaining stages go unusually well. Ask staff
to record hours by project and stage weekly; a month later, nobody remembers.

## Can Tally do this?

Tally handles the books, GST and TDS. It doesn't hold stages, hours, drawings or project margins; those usually live
in spreadsheets beside it. A practice system keeps them by project and posts invoices to Tally, so the books stay in
Tally and the project picture in one place.

## What should an architecture practice ask a software vendor?

Show me: a fee set up by stages and invoiced as each is reached; hours entered against a project and stage, and fee
earned per hour by project; a drawing register with revisions; consultants' fees on the project; and invoices posting
to Tally with GST. For firms that also execute interiors, the same system should carry quotations, purchase orders
and site work. See [ERP for interior design firms](/erp/interior-design-firms) and
[software for interior design firms](/industries/interior-design-and-furniture).
