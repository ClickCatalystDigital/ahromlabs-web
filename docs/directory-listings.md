## Long description (≈1,200 characters)

Ahrom Labs is an industrial systems engineering practice in Ahmedabad. We build the systems a business runs on: custom ERP and CRM, accounting and compliance, and AI built into those systems rather than bolted on.

Every engagement starts by mapping how the business actually works: its entities, workflows, approvals and the rules people carry in their heads. That map becomes the foundation of the system, so the software fits the business instead of the business bending to the software.

What we've built in production: a manufacturing ERP that assembles each boiler's IBR statutory folder from material test certificates and traces every plate offcut back into stock; a trading ERP that posts approved invoices into TallyPrime within 30 seconds and extracts five document types with AI at 88–95% zero-correction, each checked by a person before posting; and one shared system for two sister companies in interior design and furniture, each invoicing on its own letterhead and GSTIN.

AI in our systems follows one rule: the system suggests, a person confirms, and nothing posts to the books on a guess.

Pricing is fixed per phase, with the scope agreed in writing first. Live demos for three industries are free to try at ahromlabs.com.

## Portfolio items (one per client)

Use the illustrations from the downloads folder, or screenshots from the live demos.

### 1. LS Technologies — electronics component trading
An ERP and CRM with TallyPrime accounting automation and AI document extraction, plus a separate inventory system for PCB components and reels. Approved invoices post into TallyPrime as vouchers within 30 seconds; purchase and freight invoices, bills of entry, purchase orders and bank statements are extracted by AI at 88–95% zero-correction, each approved by a person before posting.
Demo: https://electronics-erp.ahromlabs.com/demo

### 2. Savistar & Saag — interior design and furniture (sister companies)
One system both businesses run on: workers, clients, vendors and freight, projects and site visits, workshop orders, payroll and one combined finance ledger. Each invoice uses its own company's letterhead and GSTIN; vendor and freight charges are checked against agreed rates automatically; staff see only their own cash entries.
Client quote (already published on the site): "They really took the time to understand our concerns and requirements, and the system was built the way we had envisioned it. It has brought much more structure, visibility, and control to the way we operate." — Sachi & Haripriya, owners
Demo: https://interiors-erp.ahromlabs.com/demo

### 3. A boiler and pressure-vessel manufacturer (keep unnamed)
A custom ERP, CRM and operational-intelligence system from bills of materials and material stock to IBR statutory documents. Each boiler's IBR folder is assembled from a bank of material test certificates in minutes; plate offcuts return to stock with weight derived from geometry; the system is also the book of record for GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and TDS.
Demo: https://boiler-erp.ahromlabs.com/demo
