# US manufacturing knowledge web: page architecture (Phase B)

Built 2 Nov 2026 from `kp-export.csv` (Google Keyword Planner, United States, English, Google; Oct 2025 – Sep 2026) joined to `seed-keywords.csv`. Generated by a script: every volume below is copied from the export, never typed.

**Read the numbers as brackets.** The account has no active ad spend, so Keyword Planner reports ranges and the export writes them as 50 (10–100), 500 (100–1K), 5000 (1K–10K). Cluster totals add these bracket values: good for ranking pages against each other, not a traffic forecast. Monthly columns were empty in the export.

**Competition and bids are Google Ads figures** (advertiser demand, in ₹ because that is the account currency). They say how much a click is worth to advertisers, not how hard the page is to rank organically. Ranking opportunity below is our judgement from who ranks today (sampled results pages, research.md §1), not a difficulty score.

## Scoring

Each factor 1–3. **Demand**: cluster total ≥2,000 → 3, 500–1,999 → 2, <500 → 1. **Commercial intent**: share of software/vendor phrasings and bid level. **Capability fit**: 3 = live demo or published note proves it, 2 = we can show it with a synthetic example, 1 = depends on something not built. **Ranking opportunity**: 3 = forums/thin pages rank, 2 = mixed, 1 = big vendors or listicles. **Urgency**: problem/failure wording vs learning. **Graph value**: links and terms it adds.
